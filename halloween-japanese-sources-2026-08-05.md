## 2. WHAT JAPANESE PEOPLE ACTUALLY DO ON HALLOWEEN

Based on Japanese-language sources (LiveJapan ja, note.com eyewitness accounts, fashionsnap.com 2024 report):

- **Costumes on trains**: Yes, absolutely normal on 31 Oct evening. Not on non-Halloween dates. The 0時 (midnight) trains from Shibuya/Shinjuku are packed with costumed returnees.
- **Peak timing**: People start appearing in costume around **18:00-19:00**, peak density around **21:00-23:00**, wind-down around **02:00-03:00**. First trains (04:30-05:00) are the final exit wave.
- **Where adults go**: Clubs (pre-bought tickets via iFLYER or door), izakayas in costume (staff at chains like 笑笑, 魚民 wear costumes too), karaoke (many do costume karaoke groups), and the spontaneous street gatherings (Kabukichō, and previously Shibuya).
- **Home parties**: Common among families with children and older millennials. Not the dominant adult pattern.
- **Theme parks**: Disney Halloween (Sep 17 - Nov 7 in 2025) and USJ Halloween Horror Nights are the mainstream family/couple options. Expensive.
- **Don Quijote role**: Don Quijote stores dedicate 2-3 aisles to costumes from late September. They are THE last-minute costume source. The fashionsnap.com 2024 report notes "渋谷ハロウィン当日、路上飲酒禁止条例施行も街中は混雑" — despite the ban, 15,000 people still came (down from prior 60,000 estimate).
- **Cultural note**: Japanese Halloween is fundamentally about **costume + photo + alcohol**. There is no trick-or-treat culture for adults. The costume IS the activity — people wear them to be photographed, then drink.

## 3. SHIBUYA — Legal Position (Confirmed from Ward Source)

**Source**: city.shibuya.tokyo.jp/kusei/shisaku/jorei-toshin/sbykankyo.html (last updated 2025-08-22)

**Ordinance name**: 「渋谷駅周辺地域の安全で安心な環境の確保に関する条例」(Ordinance for Securing a Safe and Secure Environment in the Area Surrounding Shibuya Station)

**Key facts**:
- Originally enacted **令和元年 (2019)** for specific event periods only.
- **Amended October 2024 (令和6年10月1日施行)**: drinking ban expanded to **year-round**, **18:00-05:00**, in public places (streets + parks) within the designated zone.
- **Zone expanded** from the original センター街/NW side to include 宮益坂 and 円山町 (club district).
- **No criminal penalty** (罰則なし) — enforcement is via patrol + verbal guidance.
- **Exemption possible**: Ward-backed events (町会, 商店会) can apply for a waiver 1 month in advance.
- **Physical measures in 2024-2025**: Hachiko statue enclosed in temporary fencing, police barriers at crossing, PA system announcements every few minutes ("立ち止まらないでください" / "路上飲酒は禁止です"), alcohol confiscation observed.
- **2025 messaging (TCC campaign)**: "禁止だよ！迷惑ハロウィーン" / "STOP TROUBLESOME HALLOWEENS" — the strongest language yet.
- **Permanent**: This is **codified municipal law**, not an annual renewal. English sources are correct.

**Effect on 2024**: Attendance dropped from ~60,000 to ~15,000 (fashionsnap.com figure). The crowd that remains is mostly gawkers/photographers, not costumed participants.

**For your plan**: Shibuya is correctly excluded. No change needed.
