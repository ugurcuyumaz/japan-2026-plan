# Japan 2026 Trip — Logistics Research
Couple, Spanish passports, Madrid residents. IB281 MAD→NRT dep 23 Oct 2026 12:30, arr 24 Oct 09:30. IB282 NRT→MAD dep 11 Nov 2026 14:10. ~18 nights on ground.

## 1. Arrival Day Mechanics at NRT

- **Terminal**: IB281 arrives at **NRT Terminal 2, 3F** international arrivals (confirmed directly on narita-airport.jp airline page). [narita-airport.jp/en/flight/airline-search/ibe](https://www.narita-airport.jp/en/flight/airline-search/ibe/)
- **JESTA is NOT live in 2026.** JESTA (Japan Electronic System for Travel Authorization) was approved by the Diet in a bill passed ~May 2026 but is targeted for rollout by **end of FY2028 (by March 2029)**. It will NOT affect this Oct/Nov 2026 trip. [timeout.com](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/news/japan-moves-to-implement-new-pre-travel-jesta-authorisation-system-from-2028-031726), [ivisa.com](https://www.ivisa.com/news/2026-05-29-jp-japan-passes-law-jesta-electronic-travel-authorization), [independent.co.uk](https://www.independent.co.uk/travel/news-and-advice/japan-jesta-visa-digital-screening-system-tourism-b2944345.html)
- **Visit Japan Web (VJW)** remains the relevant pre-arrival tool in 2026 — NOT mandatory, but strongly recommended. Register 24-48h before flight (system also allows registration any time after booking; complete ≥6h before landing). Generates a QR code covering immigration + customs (and optionally tax-free) declarations, replacing paper forms, and routes users to dedicated fast lanes. Estimated time savings: ~20-30 minutes at major airports including Narita. [wego.com](https://blog.wego.com/visit-japan-web/), [airtraveler.club](https://www.airtraveler.club/intel/visit-japan-web-speed-entry-major-airports/), [klook.com](https://www.klook.com/blog/visit-japan-web-guide/)
  - Recommendation: print/screenshot the QR codes offline in case of phone/connectivity issues. [insideosaka.com](https://insideosaka.com/a-guide-to-mysos-visit-japan-web-and-japan-entry-procedures/)
- **Immigration + baggage + customs realistic timing**: Non-US-passport (Spanish) immigration wait averages reported at 22-29 minutes depending on time of day; with VJW pre-registration this compresses toward the lower end. General live-tracking sites cite ~22 min average immigration wait, ~50 min security (not relevant on arrival) at NRT. [flightqueue.com/narita-international-airport-passport-control-wait-times](https://flightqueue.com/narita-international-airport-passport-control-wait-times), [flightqueue.com/airport/NRT](https://flightqueue.com/airport/NRT)
  - Add baggage claim (10-25 min depending on unload speed for a widebody A350 arrival) and a customs check (fast if using VJW's QR, potentially 5-15 min).
  - **Realistic total estimate wheels-down (09:30) to airside-cleared/landside with bags: ~75-120 minutes**, i.e. roughly 10:45–11:30, assuming VJW pre-registration and no unusual congestion. Early morning arrivals (this is a common wave time at NRT) can see above-average queues if several widebodies land in the same window — treat 120 min as the safer planning number, not the optimistic one.
- **NRT domestic departures**: NRT does have a domestic terminal presence, but options are much thinner than Haneda. Historically NRT domestic departures skew toward ANA/JAL regional connections and some LCCs (Peach and Jetstar Japan are based at NRT and fly domestic routes from there, e.g. to Sapporo, Fukuoka, Okinawa). ANA's own NRT domestic terminal guide confirms a domestic presence at NRT Terminal 1 satellite. [ana.co.jp/en/jp/guide/prepare/airport-guide/domestic/nrt](https://www.ana.co.jp/en/jp/guide/prepare/airport-guide/domestic/nrt/)
  - However, the **bulk of ANA/JAL domestic capacity (and best frequency to Hokkaido/Kyushu) flies out of HANEDA (HND), not Narita.** This is the classic NRT-vs-HND fork for this exact itinerary.
- **NRT ↔ HND transfer**:
  - **Fastest / simplest**: direct Airport Limousine Bus, no transfers, ~65-90 minutes, ~¥3,600 (adult one-way; figures vary ¥3,200-3,600 depending on source/date). [japlanease.com](https://japlanease.com/travel-from-narita-to-haneda/), [chillandtravel.com](https://chillandtravel.com/haneda-airport-to-narita-airport-transfers/), [blog.wego.com](https://blog.wego.com/tokyo-airport-transfer/)
  - **Train** (Keisei + transfers, or via central Tokyo): ~95-110+ minutes, cheaper (~¥1,730-2,000) but requires at least one transfer and platform-to-platform walking with luggage. [tokyocheapo.com](https://tokyocheapo.com/wp-json/wp/v2/posts/347466), [rome2rio.com](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Tokyo-Narita-Airport-NRT/Haneda-Airport-Terminal-1-2-Station)
  - **Taxi/private transfer**: ~60 min, ¥11,000-14,000+, not worth it for this itinerary.
  - **Practical implication**: even the fastest NRT→HND option costs ~1.5 hours before you're even at the HND check-in counter.

### Earliest realistic same-day domestic departure
Putting the pieces together for 24 Oct 2026 (09:30 NRT landing):
- Landside with bags at NRT: ~10:45-11:30 (optimistic-to-realistic band).
- If flying **domestic OUT OF NRT** (Peach/Jetstar Japan primarily): could realistically make an early-to-mid-afternoon flight, e.g. 13:00-14:30 departure, though NRT domestic frequency to the north (Sapporo) or south (Fukuoka) is much thinner than HND's — expect 1-3 flights/day on LCC routes from NRT, not the dozen+ ANA/JAL frequencies from HND.
- If flying **out of HND** (needed for ANA/JAL mainline domestic network, better frequency to nearly everywhere): add the ~90 min transfer + HND check-in/security buffer (domestic requires being at the gate ~30-40 min before departure, arrive at airport ~60-90 min before). Realistic earliest HND departure: **~14:30-15:30** at best, more comfortably 16:00+.
- **International→domestic connection through the SAME terminal on a single ticket is a different (faster) case** — Narita airport's own connection guide notes fixed procedures for same-day int'l→domestic connections requiring immigration first regardless. [narita-airport.jp/airportguide/connect/inter-dom](https://www.narita-airport.jp/airportguide/connect/inter-dom/) — but this trip is an independently-booked domestic leg, not a through-ticketed connection, so the couple must fully clear immigration + collect bags + then check in again for the domestic flight as ordinary passengers.

### Is a same-day domestic hop after a 14h flight a good idea?
- Forum consensus (FlyerTalk Japan connection threads, TripAdvisor Narita threads) on tight international→domestic same-day connections centers on **needing a comfortable ~3-hour buffer minimum** for immigration + bag reclaim + re-check-in, even for people NOT jetlagged. One well-regarded FlyerTalk answer: "Two hours and 45 minutes should be enough" for a through-connection scenario with priority handling — this trip's stand-alone ticket case is less forgiving, not more. [flyertalk.com](https://www.flyertalk.com/forum/japan/2175752-transfer-international-arrival-domestic-1-ticket-3-hours-narita.html)
- No traveler-reported Reddit/blog source found specifically framing "same-day domestic flight after a 14h eastbound long-haul is a bad idea" in these exact terms, but the general and consistent advice pattern for landing 09:00-ish after an overnight eastbound long-haul (Madrid→Tokyo crosses ~7 time zones) is: **jetlag hits hardest on day 1-2**, decision-making and reaction time are measurably degraded, and adding a tight connection, a second security/boarding cycle, and unfamiliar airport navigation compounds risk of missed flights or lost items. This is standard travel-medicine advice, not Japan-specific, but is a real factor for a "spend the last days in Tokyo" itinerary where flying out of order isn't actually required.

### Recommendation
- **Do NOT attempt a same-day domestic flight on arrival day.** The mechanics work only in the optimistic case (fast immigration, no delay, fast transfer if HND-bound) and any slippage cascades badly on day 1 of an 18-night trip.
- **Best plan: spend the first night near Narita/central Tokyo**, then take a domestic flight (or Shinkansen, depending on which new region is chosen) on the morning of 25 Oct. This buys:
  - A full immigration/bag/customs cycle with zero time pressure.
  - A shower, meal, and sleep before onward travel — meaningfully better for two adults after a 14h+ flight.
  - Flexibility to choose the highest-frequency domestic option (HND) the next morning rather than being squeezed into NRT's thin LCC schedule.
- If the itinerary is "new regions first, Tokyo last" (as stated), night 1 should be at a Narita-area hotel (many offer late check-in / early wake, some with shuttle) OR a central Tokyo hotel if a slightly longer transfer that evening is preferred — Narita-area is simpler logistically for a 09:30 landing.

[ana.co.jp/en/us/plan-book/promotions/special-fares](https://www.ana.co.jp/en/us/plan-book/promotions/special-fares/)

- Pricing under the (now-discontinued, but illustrative of the dynamic-pricing model that continues) Discover Japan Fare: one-way Economy roughly **USD $70-95** to major cities (Osaka, Sapporo, Fukuoka, Okinawa) from Tokyo. [therealjapan.com](https://www.therealjapan.com/ana-experience-japan-fare/)
- 2026 winter (26W) season fare charts published as PDFs show low/high season windows; for the trip window (late Oct-early Nov) fares fall in the **low-season band** based on the published Spanish-language fare table showing "Temporadas Bajas: ... 10/13~10/24" as low season, with high season limited to specific mid-summer/September/early-October date bands — late Oct/Nov reads as low season, i.e. cheaper fares. [ana.co.jp PDF](https://cad.www.ana.co.jp/www2/guide/plan/fare/domestic/fees-list/pdf/CBBG_EXST_20250909_es.pdf)
- Eligibility: foreign passport + inbound/outbound international ticket to/from Japan required (standard for all these tourist fares).
- Note: brand name and exact terms are being actively revised in 2026 — verify current product name and current fare table close to booking time, as the "Discover Japan Fare" → "Experience Japan Fare" naming transition is recent (May 2026 cutover found in search results).

### JAL "Japan Explorer Pass"
- Eligibility: passport issued by a country other than Japan (or Japanese passport holder with permanent residency abroad), AND residence outside Japan. [jal.co.jp/ar/en/jepdp/faq](https://www.jal.co.jp/ar/en/jepdp/faq/)
- Covers 30+ domestic cities, three fare tiers (Short / Middle / Long) based on route distance. Economy class only (upgrades possible standby at check-in). [jal.co.jp](https://www.jal.co.jp/br/en/guide-to-japan/plan-your-trip/tips/japan-explorer-pass.html)
- Free checked baggage included as a JAL Explorer Pass domestic-flight benefit. [jal.co.jp/idl/en/world/japan_explorer_pass/lp](https://www.jal.co.jp/idl/en/world/japan_explorer_pass/lp/)
- Note: the "$350 for 15+ days" pricing found in search results relates to a **JALPAK vacation package bundle**, not the pure Explorer Pass per-flight fare — treat that figure with caution; the Explorer Pass itself is priced per-flight/per-route-tier, not as an unlimited multi-day pass like the JR Pass. Verify current tier pricing directly at booking (fares are dynamic and season-dependent, similar to ANA's).

### Standard JAL/ANA checked baggage (domestic)
- JAL domestic: up to **20kg free per passenger** checked. [jal.co.jp/jp/en/dom/baggage/checked](https://jal.co.jp/jp/en/dom/baggage/checked/)
- ANA domestic: max linear dimensions 158cm (62in), weight cap per bag ~32kg (heavier increments charged), total baggage weight cap 100kg. Carry-on: 10kg combined for bag + personal item. [ana.co.jp baggage pages]
- Both airlines' foreigner-discount fares generally inherit the airline's standard checked-baggage allowance (not the LCC-style pay-per-kg model) — this is a real advantage over LCCs for a couple carrying normal luggage.

### LCCs (Peach, Jetstar Japan, Skymark, Air Do, Solaseed, Spring Airlines Japan)
- Route relevance for this itinerary:
  - **Hokkaido (Sapporo/New Chitose)**: Peach, Jetstar Japan, Skymark, Air Do, Spring Airlines Japan all fly Tokyo(mostly HND, some NRT for Peach/Jetstar)-Sapporo. Reported fares "sometimes under ¥5,000," especially ex-Narita. [japan-guide.com](https://s2020.japan-guide.com/e/e5306.html)
  - **Fukuoka (Kyushu gateway)**: Skymark, Peach, Jetstar Japan cited with one-way fares around **¥5,000-25,000**. [japan-guide.com](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e4802.html)
  - **Kagoshima**: primarily ANA/JAL/Solaseed mainline network; fewer LCC options — expect to use JAL Explorer Pass / ANA discount fare rather than a pure LCC here.
  - **Matsuyama (Shikoku)**: primarily ANA/JAL network from Tokyo; Shikoku has very limited LCC domestic air service — rail/ferry is often more practical for intra-Shikoku movement once there.
  - **Toyama (Japan Alps gateway)**: small ANA-served airport; LCC presence minimal — this leg is more realistically done by Hokuriku Shinkansen than by air.
- Booking horizon: no single hard rule found, but discount/dynamic fares (ANA/JAL foreigner fares) open **355 days ahead**; LCC fares are released on rolling schedules (commonly 3-11 months ahead depending on airline) and get progressively more expensive closer to departure — for late Oct/early Nov 2026 travel, booking **as early as the fare charts are published (see Peach's published "26W"/winter season fare PDFs)**, ideally 2-4 months ahead, is the practical target; earlier is safer given this is (see §6) a Culture Day long-weekend adjacent period with some domestic demand uptick.
- Baggage on LCCs: unlike JAL/ANA, expect **pay-per-kg or pay-per-bag checked baggage** (typically 15-20kg tiers, ¥1,000-3,000+ per bag if bought online in advance, more at the counter) — budget for this separately if choosing LCC routes over the foreigner-discount full-service fares.

### Nationwide JR Pass
- Current (pre-1 Oct 2026) price if bought via the **official JR Pass website**: **¥50,000 (7-day ordinary) / ¥80,000 (14-day) / ¥100,000 (21-day)**, adult, Green Car higher. [jrpass.com](https://www.jrpass.com/blog/jr-pass-price-history-how-much-the-pass-has-changed)
- **⚠️ Confirmed price increase effective 1 Oct 2026** — JR Group announced (press release 9 Apr 2026) that nationwide JR Pass prices will rise again from **1 October 2026** — this trip (23 Oct-11 Nov) falls entirely **after** this increase takes effect. [japan.travel](https://www.japan.travel/en/ca/news/price-increases-are-coming-to-the-nationwide-japan-rail-pass-2026/)
  - Nuance found in japan-guide.com's coverage: if bought via the **official JRP website**, price may stay unchanged for that channel, but purchasing through **online/offline travel agencies** will see the increase from October — i.e. buying directly from the official channel may avoid (or delay) the new higher price. Verify the exact final price and channel-dependent pricing close to purchase, as this is an active, evolving 2026 pricing situation. [japan-guide.com](https://japan-guide.com/e/e2361.html)
- **Break-even math (post-2023 hike, further worsened by the Oct 2026 hike)**: the classic break-even comparison is a Tokyo-Kyoto-Tokyo Shinkansen round trip alone (~¥27,000-28,000), meaning a 7-day pass at ¥50,000+ requires roughly **2 long-distance round trips minimum** just to break even versus buying individual tickets, and realistically 3+ substantial long-haul legs to make it clearly worthwhile post-hike. [saily.com](https://saily.com/blog/is-japan-rail-pass-worth-it/)
- For an itinerary that's **regionally clustered** (e.g. one loop up north, one loop down south, point-to-point Shinkansen only for the long inter-regional hops), the **nationwide pass is often NOT the best value anymore** post-hike — regional passes + point-to-point tickets for the 1-2 genuinely long hauls (e.g. Tokyo↔Sapporo or Tokyo↔Fukuoka, which are usually flown anyway per §2) frequently cost less in combination.
- Purchase: exchange-order/paper-voucher purchase abroad still exists as an option per JR Hokkaido's own instructions (see below), but the **primary/preferred 2026 channel is online purchase with e-ticket**, not physical vouchers — verify current status for the nationwide pass specifically at japanrailpass.net.

### Which combination makes sense
- **North-heavy itinerary (Hokkaido/Tohoku/Japan Alps)**: fly Tokyo→Sapporo (LCC or ANA/JAL discount fare, see §2) to skip the very long and pass-inefficient Tokyo-Hokkaido rail leg, then use the **JR Hokkaido Rail Pass (5 or 7-day)** for the Hokkaido loop, then either fly or take the new **JR East Pass** back through Tohoku toward Tokyo, potentially adding the **Hokuriku Arch Pass** or **Takayama-Hokuriku Pass** if routing home via the Japan Alps/Nagano corridor before returning to Tokyo. Skip the nationwide JR Pass entirely for this pattern — it's inefficient because the single longest leg (Tokyo-Sapporo) is flown, not railed.
- **South-heavy itinerary (Kyushu/Shikoku)**: fly Tokyo→Fukuoka or Tokyo→Kagoshima, then use a **JR Kyushu regional pass** (Northern/Southern/All depending on loop shape) for Kyushu, cross to Shikoku by ferry/train, use the **All Shikoku Rail Pass** there, and consider the **JR West Sanyo-San'in** or **Setouchi Area Pass** if the route swings back through Honshu via Okayama/Hiroshima before returning to Tokyo (by Shinkansen or flight).
- **Nationwide JR Pass**: only clearly worth it if the itinerary does 3+ long-distance Shinkansen legs across regions covered by the pass (e.g. Tokyo-Kyoto-Hiroshima-Tokyo) rather than flying the long inter-region hops — given this itinerary explicitly wants to reach Hokkaido/Kyushu/Shikoku (all far from Tokyo), **flying the long hop + regional pass at the destination is very likely cheaper and faster than a nationwide pass**, especially post the 1 Oct 2026 price hike.

### Where to buy in 2026
- Regional passes: primarily bought online in advance (e-ticket exchange order) or at station counters/travel-agent counters in Japan; JR Hokkaido explicitly notes physical station purchase requires cash (JPY only) or credit card — **Apple Pay/mobile wallets NOT accepted** for in-person rail-pass purchase. [jrhokkaido.co.jp](https://www.jrhokkaido.co.jp/global/english/ticket/railpass/)
- Nationwide JR Pass: verify current online-only vs paper-voucher status close to purchase — multiple 2023-2026 changes to this process have occurred industry-wide.

## 4. Ground Logistics

### Luggage forwarding (takuhaibin / Yamato "TA-Q-BIN")
- Widely available at Narita/Haneda arrivals via Yamato Transport counters (look for the black-cat yellow-oval logo). No advance reservation — walk up on arrival. [isfrski.org](https://isfrski.org/resources/Documents/Ski%20Weeks/Japan%202026/Luggage%20shipping%20in%20Japan.pdf)
- 2026 pricing: airport-origin surcharge **¥660/parcel** included in quoted rates; typical international-arrival Tokyo airport→hotel rates range from **~¥2,000 (local Tokyo) to ¥4,700 (Okinawa)**; next-day delivery standard for most mainland destinations. [airtraveler.club](https://www.airtraveler.club/intel/japan-luggage-airport-hotel-13/), [kuronekoyamato.co.jp](https://www.kuronekoyamato.co.jp/ytc/en/send/services/airport/)
- A **"Round Trip TA-Q-BIN"** service also exists — drop bags at accommodation, no upfront paperwork/payment, usable up to 1 month later — useful for "send luggage back to Tokyo hotel for pickup after the regional loop" pattern. [kuronekoyamato.co.jp](https://www.kuronekoyamato.co.jp/ytc/en/send/services/bothways/)
- **Practical use for this itinerary**: forward the bulk of luggage from the Tokyo/Narita area hotel directly to the LAST Tokyo hotel (to be picked up at the end of the regional loop), carrying only a smaller bag for the ~2-week regional trip — very common pattern, low risk, low cost relative to the convenience gained. Lead time: budget 1-2 days for delivery, so timed drop-offs need planning around exact hotel dates.

### Coin lockers
- Widely available at major stations/airports; the app **"Coin Locker Navi"** helps locate available lockers in real time — useful in dense stations (Tokyo, Shinjuku, Shin-Osaka) where lockers fill up fast. No 2026-specific pricing change found; expect ¥300-700/day depending on locker size and station.

### IC cards (Suica/Pasmo) — chip shortage resolved
- The 2023-2024 global semiconductor shortage that suspended sales of **unnamed/anonymous physical Suica and Pasmo cards** is confirmed **resolved as of 2026**. [unseen-japan.com](https://unseen-japan.com/suica-pasmo-physical-cards-sale/), [voyapon.com](https://voyapon.com/suica-mobile-guide-japan/), [livejapan.com](https://livejapan.com/en/article-a0002783/)

### ⚠️ Tax-free shopping — major rule change takes effect DURING this trip

## 5. Rental Car

### The IDP question for Spanish licence holders — VERIFIED, no trap here
- **Spain IS a signatory to the 1949 Geneva Convention on Road Traffic**, and an International Driving Permit issued by the Spanish authority (DGT) in the correct 1949-Geneva format **IS valid to drive in Japan.** This is confirmed by multiple independent official-adjacent sources: [kart.st](https://kart.st/en/drivers-license/spain.html) ("An international driving permit (IDP) under the 1949 Geneva Convention in the proper format issued by the official agent or agency appointed by Spain is valid to drive in Japan"), and the Tokyo Metropolitan Police FAQ confirming only 1949-Geneva-format IDPs (not 1968 Vienna Convention format) are valid regardless of issuing country. [tokyo.lg.jp / keishicho](https://www.keishicho.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/multilingual/english/finding_services/faq/drivers_license/drivers_license01.html)
- **No evidence found of Spain being an exception or having a special problem** in the way this research brief flagged as a "known trap for some EU countries" (that trap more commonly applies to countries whose IDPs are issued under the 1968 Vienna Convention format instead of 1949 Geneva — e.g. some sources note this affects certain countries differently). Spain's DGT-issued IDP is Geneva-format and works.
- **IDP validity window**: valid for driving in Japan for **up to 1 year from the date of entry into Japan**, as long as the IDP itself (expires 1 year from issuance) hasn't expired. [pref.chiba.jp](https://www.police.pref.chiba.jp/english/International_license.html), [hyogo.lg.jp](https://www.police.pref.hyogo.lg.jp/traffic/license/japan/data/English.pdf)

### Where/how to get the IDP in Spain
- Issued by the **DGT (Dirección General de Tráfico)**.
- Cost: **~€10.51** per the DGT official fee cited in a 2023 news source — verify current fee at booking time as DGT fees are periodically revised, but this remains the right ballpark. [elnortedecastilla.es](https://www.elnortedecastilla.es/sociedad/dgt-trafico-como-conseguir-el-permiso-de-conducir-internacional-tramites-y-precio-20230312194713-nt.html)
- Process: apply at a DGT Jefatura/Oficina de Tráfico (in-person or via DGT's electronic sede in some cases) — bring passport photo, existing Spanish licence, and pay the fee; typically issued same-day or within days at the counter (not a multi-week wait like license renewals). Recommend applying well before departure (weeks, not days) to avoid any counter/appointment scheduling friction.

### Museum/attraction closures
- General rule in Japan: many museums and some traditional gardens/attractions close on **Mondays** (or the following weekday if Monday is a holiday) — this is the most common single closure day pattern nationwide, though NOT universal (some close Tuesdays instead, especially certain municipal museums). **Because Culture Day (3 Nov) is a Tuesday holiday, any attraction with a "closed Wednesdays if Tuesday is a holiday" substitute-closure rule could shift its closure to Wednesday 4 Nov** — this is a common Japanese closure-shift convention and should be checked per-attraction once specific museums are on the itinerary.

### Typhoon season tail risk, late October
- Typhoon formation frequency by month (JMA 1991-2020 averages): **July 3.7, August 5.7, September 5.0, October 3.4** typhoons forming on average — October is meaningfully lower than the Aug/Sep peak but **not zero**. [weather-jwa.jp](https://weather-jwa.jp/en/news/articles/post6763)
- Most typhoons that actually **approach/make landfall in Japan** (as opposed to just forming somewhere in the Pacific) cluster **July-October**, with the most damaging historical typhoons in the modern (Heisei-era) record specifically landing **September-October** — autumn typhoons are noted as unusually damaging because they can trigger stationary autumn rain fronts (aki same) causing severe flooding. [mainichi.jp](https://mainichi.jp/english/articles/20220915/p2a/00m/0op/018000c), [wagaya-japan.com](https://wagaya-japan.com/en/journal_detail.php?id=10455)
- **By early-to-mid November, typhoon risk drops sharply** — the season is considered to be tailing off by then, though a late-October typhoon brushing Japan is a real, non-negligible possibility (not "typhoon season is over" by 24 Oct — closer to "typhoon season is winding down but not finished").
- **Practical implication**: build a small amount of schedule slack around any critical single-day activities (e.g. a rural ferry connection, a mountain cable car) in the **first week of the trip (24-31 Oct)** specifically, since that's still nominally within typhoon season, even though the statistical frequency is already declining from the Aug/Sep peak.

### Sunrise/sunset & short days

Recommend confirming exact sunrise/sunset for the actual chosen dates/cities via timeanddate.com once the day-by-day itinerary is drafted, and planning outdoor/scenic activities (koyo viewing especially) for midday rather than assuming long evening light.
