# Rail Pass — decision of record

## Buy: JR East–South Hokkaido Rail Pass, ¥40,000 pp. Activate 26 October.

**Six CONSECUTIVE calendar days**, midnight to midnight → **covers 26–31 October.**

⚠️ **There is no flexible-day version.** Reseller listings advertising "6 flexible days within 14" are stale — the flexible product was discontinued in March 2026. Verified against japan-guide, page updated 2026-03-14. So idle days can't be skipped over.

---

## Why that window

It's an exact fit for the leg's expensive rail:

| Date | Leg | ¥ pp |
|---|---|---|
| 26 Oct | Hokuto, Sapporo → Noboribetsu | 4,890 |
| 27 Oct | Hokuto, Noboribetsu → Hakodate | 7,790 |
| 29 Oct | Local to Shin-Hakodate-Hokuto | 440 |
| 29 Oct | Hayabusa 18 → Sendai | 18,300 |
| 30 Oct | Senseki Line, Matsushima return | ~840 |
| 31 Oct | Hayabusa, Sendai → Tokyo | 11,410 |

Only the two cheapest legs fall outside: the **¥1,150 airport rapid on the 24th** and the **Narita transfer on 11 Nov**. Both cash.

**Saving: roughly €50–60 for the couple.** Thin — but free unlimited seat reservations on two all-reserved trains and two Shinkansen carry their own weight, and it covers JR East local lines in Tokyo on Halloween Saturday.

---

## Why not the cheaper ¥32,000 Tohoku–South Hokkaido pass

Its area is defined prefecture by prefecture — Fukushima, Miyagi, Yamagata, Akita, Iwate, Aomori, plus southern Hokkaido. **No Kanto, no Tokyo.** Sendai→Tokyo is only covered as far as Fukushima Prefecture, so you'd pay the out-of-area stretch south of there at the gate — landing within a few hundred yen of the ¥40,000 pass.

**A dead heat on price, so take the ¥40,000 one on simplicity:** no boundary fare adjustment, free reservations throughout, and Tokyo local lines included.

---

## Nikko is not covered

Not in this window. **Buy the Tobu limited express tickets in cash**, ¥3,050–3,940 pp each way — Revaty at the bottom of that range, SPACIA X at the top. It's the train, not the time of day: Japanese rail fares are regulated with **no time-of-day or demand pricing and no advance-purchase discount**. Booking early buys a seat, not a better price.

*Worth knowing for the Tokyo hotel choice: there is a **direct limited express to Nikko from Shinjuku**, not just from Asakusa. A pass window reaching 2 November could cover it — but only by sacrificing both Hokuto legs (¥12,680) to gain ~¥3,500. About ¥9,000 worse. Rejected.*

## Buying and collecting

**Buy mid-August to late September**, on **JR East's own site**:
- Before the **1 October price revision**
- An exchange order is **valid three months from issue**, so an August purchase still covers a 26 October activation

**Collect at a passport-reader machine at Narita**, T1 or T2, on arrival morning — budget it into the 2.5–3.5 h you're already allowing.

**Seat reservations open one month ahead.** The two Hokuto legs are mandatory, not optional — that train has **no unreserved cars at all**. See `04-bookings/booking-calendar.md`.
