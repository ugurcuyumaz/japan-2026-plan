## The two onsen nights — why they work

This is the constraint that has governed every accommodation decision on the trip: **a locked door means the tattoo question never comes up.**

**Casual Euro, Nikko.** The hotel's own site states **全客室 露天風呂付** — *every* room has an open-air bath, and it is the **only** bath in the room. There is **no public bath anywhere** on the property. So the ¥11,000 category is exactly as private as the ¥19,080 Standard Twin, and saves **€43** on the single night.
⚠️ It showed **"1 room left"** when checked — the scarcest inventory on the trip.

**Noboribetsu Grand Hotel.** ✅ **The kashikiri bath is CONFIRMED from the hotel's own site** (`nobogura.co.jp`, FAQ + hot-spring pages): 温泉家族風呂 — **¥4,400 / 50 min, up to 4 people, 2 washing areas, 要予約 (reservation compulsory)**, spring type 食塩泉 source-fed flow-through. The FAQ also states 家族⾵呂は**1箇所しかございません** — **one unit only**, so slots are cleaned between bookings and it is not first-come.

⚠️ **Call +81-143-84-2101 to reserve the 26 Oct slot** — not to ask whether it exists. Booking the room does not book the bath, and one unit across a whole hotel is the constraint.

*Only the Japanese pages carry this; the English site mentions rooms with open-air baths and nothing else, which is why three earlier English-only passes came back empty.*

### Upgrade path for Noboribetsu

All three alternatives are researched and all have a private bath.

| | All-in | Trade |
|---|---|---|
| **Sekisuitei お任せ** | €177 | **+€22 buys five kashikiri baths** (¥3,300, 15:00–23:00) instead of one. Hotel picks your room — could be small or smoking |
| Sekisuitei standard 和室 | €212 | +€57, specified room, no smoking risk |
| **Takinoya Bekkan Tamanoyu** | €217 | +€62, **outdoor** sulphur kashikiri, intimate 24-room ryokan. No in-room bath — 50 min is your only soak |

⚠️ **Sekisuitei quotes PER PERSON** — the correction that reshaped this section — and **has no room-only rate**; half-board is enforced on every platform. Its dinner is a **buffet, not kaiseki**. Noboribetsu Onsen has only five or six restaurants total, so half-board is right regardless.

⚠️ **VERIFY before booking Sekisuitei:** that the kashikiri baths are **locked rooms, not curtained**. Five independent rooms strongly implies locked, but the whole tattoo strategy rests on it.

**Rejected:** Lake Toya — budget hotels there are communal-bath only, and the one property with a private bath costs *more* plus a 26-minute bus each way. Turtle Inn Nikko — cheaper but 3 km out. Hotel Mahoroba — cheaper still and reportedly tattoo-friendly, but that policy comes from a third-party site, which reverses the private-bath principle.

## The Tokyo week — Tokyu Stay Monzen-Nakacho

### Why an apart-hotel and not an Airbnb

| | 7 nights | Kitchen | Laundry | Front desk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Toyoko Inn class | €372–481 | ✗ | ✗ | ✅ |
| Airbnb (e.g. Stylewood Sangenjaya) | €394–569 | ✅ | washer, **no dryer** | ✗ |
| **Tokyu Stay Monzen-Nakacho** | **€430–903** | ✅ | **washer-dryer in room** ⚠️ *select units only* | ✅ |
| MIMARU / MONday | €613–1,094 | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

**You arrive on day 12 of an 18-night trip, so laundry is the point, not a luxury.** An Airbnb washer with no dryer means three to four hours of bathroom-ventilation drying. A front desk also means bag storage on the midday arrival from Nikko, before the 15:00 check-in.

⚠️ **The minpaku trap, if you reconsider an Airbnb.** **M-prefix** listings are capped at 180 nights a year **and** subject to ward weekday bans — **Shinjuku bans minpaku Monday to Friday** in residential zones, and your week is Wed→Tue, almost all weekdays. That profile is exactly what gets cancelled on arrival. **Non-M listings under the hotel licence (旅館業法) have no such limits**; every apart-hotel chain holds that licence. June 2026 also gave wards power to ban minpaku outright. If you do want an Airbnb, the permissive wards are **Koto, Sumida and Taito** — the same east side as the workshops — and you want a "Licensed Vacation Rental" badge with a non-M number.

### Why Monzen-Nakacho

Chosen on two independent grounds — izakaya density and week-long accommodation value. That convergence is the strongest convergence on any decision in this trip.

**Izakaya, which was the stated deciding criterion.** Old shitamachi around Tomioka Hachiman: **Tatsumi Shindō yokocho**, **Uosan Sakaba** (trading since 1949), **Takioka** standing bar, **Orihara Shōten** kakuuchi. **¥3,000–6,000 an evening for two.** Effectively no tourists, and — unlike Jimbocho, the runner-up — **open seven days**.

**Transit, which decides everything else:**

| To | Time |
|---|---|
| **Tokyo Station** — Kamakura day trip | **5 min** |
| **Asakusa** — Tobu Nikko line, and the workshops | **11 min** |
| **Shinjuku** — Shimokitazawa, Map Camera | 15 min |
| Jimbocho · Kitasenju | 10 min each |
| Akabane | 20 min |
| **Narita** — TYO-NRT bus from **Tokyo Station Yaesu South Exit**, ¥1,500 pp | **75 min** |

It's on the **Toei Oedo line**, which also runs direct to **Roppongi** — which is what makes the Halloween extension below possible.

## ✅ DECIDED: the two Halloween nights go in east Shinjuku

**31 Oct and 1 Nov in east Shinjuku; 3–10 Nov at Tokyu Stay Monzen-Nakacho.** Two bookings.

**What forced it:** the Halloween night shape was decided the same day as **Shape B — Kōenji then WARP Shinjuku** — and Shape B only pays for itself if you can **walk home from WARP at 03:00**. From Monzen-Nakacho you'd pay a **¥5,000–6,000 taxi** in the 00:30–05:00 rail gap with a 15–40 min wait, which erases most of the €45–90 the shape saves over the Roppongi route. Kōenji is also **8 min from Shinjuku against 30 from Koto**. Add the 5-minute walk to Don Quijote and the 10 to Ni-chome as a fallback, and the case is one-sided.

⚠️ **The price remains ASSUMED** — ¥18,000–26,000 for the Halloween Saturday and ¥12,000–18,000 for the Sunday, inflated by a Kabukicho Halloween premium. **A placeholder, not a quote**; 2026 Halloween rates are not bookable yet. Roppongi skews luxury; Tokyo Station is atmosphere-free business hotels; **avoid west Shinjuku**, dead after dark.

**Three real properties in the right spot** — normal rate, then the Halloween-Saturday estimate:

| | Walk to DonQui | Normal twin | 31 Oct est. | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tokyu Stay Shinjuku Eastside** | 6 min | ¥14,000–20,000 | ¥20,000–28,000 | ⭐ **value pick — same chain, washer-dryer for a 1 Nov wash** |
| **APA Higashi-Shinjuku Kabukicho Tower** | 8 min | ¥14,000–22,000 | ¥20,000–30,000 | cheapest, **4 min to Higashi-Shinjuku on the Oedo line** — the 2 Nov transfer |
| **Shinjuku Granbell Hotel** | 3 min | ¥15,000–23,000 | ¥22,000–35,000 | closest to Kabukicho, most exposed to the premium |

**Tokyu Stay Shinjuku Eastside is the pick.** Same chain as the Monzen-Nakacho booking, the washer-dryer covers a mid-trip wash on the recovery day, and it is inside the 03:00 walk-home radius from WARP.

### The cost of splitting, and how it's handled

The single-booking alternative — all ten Tokyo nights at Monzen-Nakacho — would have given one unpack and no luggage move. **Splitting means checking out on the morning of 2 Nov with two large cases while you go to Nikko with day bags.** Two mechanisms, in order of preference:

1. **Ride them over — free.** Shinjuku → **Monzen-Nakacho is direct on the Oedo line**, ~20 min, no transfers. Drop at Tokyu Stay ~08:30, then Monzen-Nakacho → Asakusa is 11 min for the 10:00 Tobu. ⚠️ **Needs Tokyu Stay to accept a drop the day before check-in** — question 3 below.
2. **Takkyubin — ¥2,000–4,000.** Ship from the Shinjuku front desk on the **afternoon of 1 Nov**, the recovery day when you're doing nothing, for 2 Nov delivery. Tokyu Stay holds it until the evening of the 3rd.

**Cost is roughly a wash either way:** the two assumed Shinjuku nights (€162–237) replace two nights at Monzen-Nakacho, and the transfer is either free or €11–22.

**The rejected alternative, for the record: one property for all ten Tokyo nights.** Two bookings at Monzen-Nakacho (31 Oct–1 Nov, then 3–10 Nov) with the cases held across the 2 Nov gap. It would have given **one unpack, no luggage move and no takkyubin question** — genuinely tidier. It lost on the Halloween night alone: **Kōenji 30 min instead of 8, and a ¥5,000–6,000 taxi home at 03:00 instead of a walk.** If you later switch away from Shape B, this decision should be revisited, because the Roppongi route needs a taxi home from *either* base and the Shinjuku advantage shrinks to the Don Quijote and Ni-chome walks.
