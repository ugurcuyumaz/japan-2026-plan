**For:** Spanish couple (2 adults, Madrid), 2nd Japan trip. Land NRT T2 09:30 Sat 24 Oct 2026; must be in Tokyo by mid-morning Sat 31 Oct 2026 (Halloween).
**Hard constraints:** no rental car; no serious hiking (city walking / flat strolls / ropeways OK); both have tattoos (private-bath onsen only — see companion doc `onsen-tattoo-hokkaido.md` for the full tattoo/onsen shortlist, not repeated here); autumn foliage not a priority, but flag anything actually closed.

## 1. Arrival day, 24 October — NRT to Sapporo

### 1.1 Which airlines fly NRT→CTS, and is a same-day early-afternoon flight realistic?

NRT–CTS (Narita to New Chitose) is a **domestic-fare route operated by LCCs plus ANA** — it is genuinely a different route from HND–CTS (which JAL and ANA fly heavily). Confirmed carriers on NRT–CTS: **ANA (NH), Peach (MM), Jetstar Japan (GK), Spring Airlines Japan (IJ)** — 4 airlines, ~123 weekly departures combined ([flyteam.jp](https://flyteam.jp/en/airline_route/nrt_cts), [directflights.com](https://www.directflights.com/NRT-CTS)). JAL does **not** operate NRT–CTS — its Sapporo flights from Tokyo go via Haneda, not Narita (confirmed: JAL's own NRT→Sapporo fare search returns "no fares matching your criteria") ([jal.co.jp](https://www.jal.co.jp/domestic/ja-jp/flights-from-tokyo-narita-to-sapporo-new-chitose)).

- **Departure time band:** Overall NRT–CTS departures range 06:50–18:15 across the day depending on airline/season ([directflights.com](https://www.directflights.com/NRT-CTS)). We found one concretely time-stamped Jetstar Japan flight, **GK105, departing NRT 09:35** ([flightmapper.net](http://info.flightmapper.net/ja/flight/Jetstar_Japan_GK_105)) — too early for a 09:30 landing. Exact flight-by-flight schedules for late Oct 2026 were not published far enough in advance to pin a specific 13:00–16:00 flight number at the time of this research; airlines typically release schedules ~10–11 months out, so a 13:30–15:00 departure will very likely exist (all four airlines fly this route multiple times daily) but must be confirmed on the actual airline booking engines closer to the date, or via Google Flights/Skyscanner once the Oct 2026 schedule loads.
- **Flight duration:** ~1h35–1h45 typically cited for NRT–CTS-class routes ([flightroutes.com](https://www.flightroutes.com/NRT-CTS) cites 1h45 shortest for the wider Tokyo–Sapporo market; short-haul narrow-body domestic routes in Japan commonly run 1h35).
- **Fare (late Oct, one-way, LCC economy):** Skyscanner shows fares from **£52 (~¥10,000)** on Spring Japan for this exact route in comparable off-peak periods ([skyscanner.net](https://skyscanner.net/routes/nrt/cts/tokyo-narita-to-sapporo-chitose.html)) — expect roughly ¥8,000–20,000 one-way per person for LCC economy booked a few months out; ANA will be pricier (¥15,000–30,000+ class, similar to its HND–CTS fares which start around ¥11,490–20,504 in comparable searches ([ana.co.jp](https://www.ana.co.jp/en-jp/flights-from-hakodate-to-tokyo))).
- **Terminal:** ANA and JAL-affiliated flights use **NRT Terminal 1**; the budget carriers relevant here — **Jetstar Japan and Spring Airlines Japan use NRT Terminal 3**; **Peach also generally uses Terminal 3** at Narita. Since the couple lands at **Terminal 2**, a Peach/Jetstar/Spring domestic flight from T3 requires an inter-terminal transfer (shuttle bus, ~8–12 min T1↔T2, similar order for T2↔T3) ([narita-airport.jp](https://www.narita-airport.jp/airportguide/connect/inter-inter/)) on top of the immigration/customs/check-in chain below. **ANA's NRT domestic departures are from Terminal 1**, also requiring a T2→T1 transfer bus.

### 1.2 Minimum connection time, international arrival (T2) → domestic departure

Narita's own published guidance for an **international-to-domestic self-transfer through Terminal 2** is a **60-minute** process (deplane → baggage claim in the domestic arrival lobby on 2F → immigration/security in the transit zone → to the domestic departure gate on 2F) — this is the officially stated minimum by JAL's airport guide for T2 international→domestic transfers ([jal.co.jp](https://www.jal.co.jp/content/dam/assets/en/inter/airport/nrt/dom/index.html)). Separately, for a genuine international-arrival passenger (not already checked through), the real chain is: **deplane → immigration → baggage claim → customs → (if domestic flight is a different terminal) inter-terminal bus → re-check-in / security for domestic flight.**

Realistic timing components, sourced:
- **Immigration wait:** non-US/Japanese passport holders average **22–29 minutes**; can spike higher at peak times ([flightqueue.com](https://flightqueue.com/narita-international-airport-passport-control-wait-times)). Narita's own site notes actual congestion can exceed the displayed estimate ([narita-airport.jp](https://www.narita-airport.jp/airportguide/inter-arr/immigration/)).
- **Baggage claim:** typically 15–30 min from touchdown depending on aircraft/stand, not separately time-stamped in our sources but standard for a widebody long-haul arrival.
- **Security (if re-clearing for the domestic flight from a different terminal/counter):** average ~50 minutes, up to 75+ at peak hours 05:00–08:00 and 16:00–19:00 ([flightqueue.com](https://flightqueue.com/narita-international-airport-security-wait-times)) — but this figure is for the *international* departure security lines; a domestic-flight security line at T1/T3 is typically much shorter and faster than this, though no domestic-specific wait-time source was found.
- **Inter-terminal transfer bus (T2→T1 or T2→T3):** ~8–12 minutes for T1↔T2; comparable for T2↔T3 ([narita-airport.jp](https://www.narita-airport.jp/airportguide/connect/inter-inter/)).

**Verdict:** Landing at 09:30, realistically **budget 2.5–3.5 hours end-to-end** for a genuine international-arrival, self-transfer, different-terminal domestic connection at Narita — this comfortably clears immigration/bags/customs/terminal-transfer/check-in by roughly **12:00–13:00**, which makes a **13:30–15:00 NRT–CTS departure realistic but tight-to-comfortable, not generously cushioned**. It is **not** the kind of margin you'd want on a delayed inbound flight. Given the couple's flight arrives at 09:30 (a full 4+ hours ahead of a 13:30 departure), this is workable, but we would recommend targeting the **14:00–16:00 band rather than 13:00–13:30** to absorb immigration queue variance, and pre-booking baggage claim/customs-savvy (nothing to declare, e-visa/QR if using Visit Japan Web) to shave 10–20 minutes.
**Specific flight to book:** without a live Oct-2026 schedule yet available, the actionable guidance is: **book ANA (Terminal 1) if you want fewer moving parts and a same-terminal-family experience with Star Alliance status recognition**, or **Peach/Jetstar/Spring (Terminal 3) for the cheapest fare** if the extra T2→T3 bus doesn't concern you. Re-check exact flight numbers/times on Google Flights or the airline's own booking engine ~3–4 months before travel once the season is loaded (ANA/JAL typically load schedules ~330 days out, i.e. around Nov 2025 for Oct 2026 — should already be bookable now).

### 1.3 CTS airport → Sapporo city

**JR Rapid Airport** is the standard, and by far the fastest and most frequent, option:
- **Journey time:** ~33–40 minutes to Sapporo Station (37 min average cited) ([jrhokkaido.co.jp](https://www.jrhokkaido.co.jp/global/english/travel/airport.html), [japan-guide.com](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e6705.html)).
- **Fare:** ¥1,150 one-way in most recent sources (older sources cite ¥1,000–1,150; use ¥1,150 as the current planning figure — [japan-guide.com](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e6705.html) cites the Otaru-continuation fare structure consistent with this).
- **Frequency:** roughly every **10–15 minutes** during the day (6×/hour cited by one 2026-dated source) — no reservation needed for standard seating; reserved "u-seat" cars available for extra luggage space at a small supplement ([navitime.com](https://japantravel.navitime.com/en/area/jp/guide/NTJjtg2601036-en/), [d-japantravel.com](https://d-japantravel.com/travel/chitose-sapporo-access)).
- Ticket machines and staffed counters are at New Chitose Airport Station, directly connected to the terminal.

**Practical arrival-day plan:** land CTS ~15:15–17:00 (depending on the exact NRT departure chosen) → JR Rapid Airport (~35 min, no reservation needed) → Sapporo Station by ~16:00–17:45 → check into hotel, first Sapporo dinner that evening (soup curry or jingisukan — see Section 4).

### Route Option A — Sapporo → Otaru (day trip) → Noboribetsu (1 night) → Hakodate (2 nights), back through Sapporo not required

- Day 1 (24 Oct, arrival): CTS → Sapporo, evening in Sapporo.
- Days 2–3 (25–26 Oct): Sapporo base, day trip to **Otaru** (round trip ~1h in transit total), full second day in Sapporo city.
- Day 4 (27 Oct): Sapporo → Noboribetsu (~1h10 transit + 15 min bus = ~1.5h of the day), afternoon/evening in Jigokudani + onsen night (Bourou Noguchi / Takinoya, per companion doc).
- Day 5 (28 Oct): Noboribetsu → Hakodate (~2h15–2h30 transit), afternoon arrival, Hakodate evening (morning market is AM-only, so plan for day 6).
- Days 6–7 (29–30 Oct): Full Hakodate days — morning market, Mt Hakodate ropeway, Motomachi, Goryokaku, Onuma day trip.
- Day 7 evening / Day 8 AM (31 Oct): depart Hakodate area for Tokyo (see Section 5).

**Transit burden:** Day 1 ~1h, Day 4 ~1.5h, Day 5 ~2.5h, Day 8 (departure) 4h11m if Shinkansen or ~1h20 if flying — moderate, front-loaded.

### Route Option B — Sapporo → Jozankei (1 night, near-Sapporo onsen) → Otaru (day trip from Sapporo) → Hakodate (3 nights direct)

- Day 1 (24 Oct): CTS → Sapporo.
- Day 2 (25 Oct): Sapporo → Jozankei (~1h bus), onsen night at Jozankei Tsuruga Mori no Uta.
- Day 3 (26 Oct): Jozankei → Sapporo (~1h bus back), afternoon in Sapporo.
- Day 4 (27 Oct): Otaru day trip from Sapporo (round trip ~1–1.5h transit), back to Sapporo for the night.
- Day 5 (28 Oct): Sapporo → Hakodate direct (~3h30 Hokuto), afternoon/evening in Hakodate.
- Days 6–7 (29–30 Oct): Full Hakodate days including Onuma.
- Day 8 AM (31 Oct): depart for Tokyo.

**Transit burden:** More total travel days touched by transit (4 out of 7), but each leg is shorter — this avoids the multi-hour Noboribetsu detour and keeps the single long-haul leg (Sapporo–Hakodate) as one clean 3h30 block. **This option skips a dedicated Noboribetsu overnight** in favor of Jozankei's much shorter, near-Sapporo access — worth considering if minimizing total transit hours matters more than experiencing Noboribetsu's Jigokudani up close (Jozankei has no comparable hell-valley feature, but Jigokudani is still visitable as an extended day trip from Sapporo via the same route as Option A, just compressed).

### Route Option C — Sapporo (city-heavy) → Noboribetsu (1 night) → Hakodate (3 nights), Otaru as a half-day only

- Day 1 (24 Oct): CTS → Sapporo.
- Days 2–3 (25–26 Oct): 1.5 days deep in Sapporo (all major sights), half-day Otaru add-on on day 3 afternoon.
- Day 4 (27 Oct): Sapporo → Noboribetsu, onsen night.
- Day 5 (28 Oct): Noboribetsu → Hakodate.
- Days 6–7 (29–30 Oct): Hakodate + Onuma day trip.
- Day 8 AM (31 Oct): depart for Tokyo.

**This is essentially Option A with Otaru compressed to a half-day**, freeing more time in Sapporo itself — recommended if the couple cares more about Sapporo's food scene/city sights than a full leisurely Otaru day. See Section 8 for our final pick, which is a refined version of this.

## 4. Food — specific recommendations

Note on crab: **crab is NOT at its peak in late October.** Multiple sources confirm Hokkaido's major crab species peak in **winter** — Red King Crab (Tarabagani) is best **November–February** (and April–May) ([tsunagujapan.com](https://www.tsunagujapan.com/wow_02064/), [savorjapan.com](https://savorjapan.com/contents/discover-oishii-japan/indulge-in-crab-japan-s-ultimate-winter-luxury-food)); the one exception is **Hanasaki crab, in season August–October** ([japan.travel](https://www.japan.travel/en/sg/guide/cheap-and-delicious-seafood-hokkaido/)) — so if the couple wants crab, ask specifically for **Hanasaki crab**, still in its window at the very end of Oct, rather than expecting peak king/snow crab. Salmon and ikura (salmon roe), by contrast, **are** in season September–November, so this is actually an excellent window for salmon/roe dishes ([myeasternhokkaido.com](http://www.myeasternhokkaido.com/blog/hokkaido-seafood)).

## 5. Getting to Tokyo on the morning of 31 October

### Option (a): Fly Hakodate (HKD) → Haneda (HND)

- Direct flights are operated (priceline notes nonstop HKD–HND typically **1h20–1h30**; connecting via CTS extends to 3–4h — avoid connections) ([priceline.com](https://www.priceline.com/flight-routes/en-us/HKD/TYO/flights-from-hakodate-to-tokyo.ssp)).
- flightconnections.com's HKD→HND data (albeit generic/aggregate, not Oct-2026-specific) shows **~8 flights/day, departures spread 08:00–19:30** ([flightconnections.com](https://www.flightconnections.com/flights-from-hkd-to-hnd)) — this suggests an early-morning HKD departure (e.g., 08:00-ish) is realistic, which would land in Haneda by ~09:30, giving the earliest arrival into Tokyo of all three options.
- **Fare:** ANA one-way fares in comparable searches start around **¥20,504–25,190** ([ana.co.jp](https://www.ana.co.jp/ja-jp/flights-from-hakodate-to-tokyo)); budget-carrier or off-peak one-ways can run lower (Skyscanner shows round-trip fares from **£99** in general searches, implying roughly £50/one-way-equivalent in the cheapest cases) ([skyscanner.net](https://www.skyscanner.net/routes/hkd/hnd/hakodate-to-tokyo-haneda.html)).
- Haneda→central Tokyo: Tokyo Monorail or Keikyu line to Hamamatsucho/Shimbashi, ~15–30 minutes — Haneda is notably closer to central Tokyo than Narita.

### Option (b): Fly Sapporo New Chitose (CTS) → Haneda (HND)

- This is a **much higher-frequency trunk route** — flyteam notes the *last* direct CTS→HND flight of the day departs 21:45, implying many flights across the day ([flyteam.jp](https://flyteam.jp/en/airline_route/cts_hnd)); both ANA and JAL run this route heavily (fares from ¥11,490 HND→CTS cited by JAL for the reverse direction, similarly priced both ways) ([jal.co.jp](https://www.jal.co.jp/domestic/ja-jp/flights-from-tokyo-haneda-to-sapporo-new-chitose)).
- Flight time ~1h30. Well-served with early-morning departures (this route has some of the most frequent domestic schedules in Japan).
- **This option only makes sense if the itinerary's last night is in/near Sapporo, not Hakodate** — since our recommended routing (Section 8) ends in Hakodate, Option (b) would require backtracking to Sapporo first, adding ~3h30 of Hokuto/train time the night before, which defeats the purpose. **Not recommended for a Hakodate-based final leg.**

### Option (c): Hokkaido/Tohoku Shinkansen, Shin-Hakodate-Hokuto → Tokyo

- Confirmed timetable (Hayabusa services): departures at **07:38 (arr. 12:08, 4h30m)**, **09:35 (arr. 14:08, 4h33m)**, **10:53 (arr. 15:04, 4h11m)**, **12:48 (arr. 17:04, 4h16m)** ([ekitan.com](https://www.ekitan.com/en/planner/shinkansen/section/shin-hakodate-hokuto/tokyo)).
- **Fare:** reserved seat ¥24,000–24,200 one-way; Green Car ¥32,870–33,070; GranClass ¥43,870–44,070 ([ekitan.com](https://www.ekitan.com/en/planner/shinkansen/section/shin-hakodate-hokuto/tokyo/hayabusa)).
- The **earliest train (07:38) arrives Tokyo Station 12:08** — this is midday, not mid-morning, and Shin-Hakodate-Hokuto Station itself is a further ~20 minutes by local train from central Hakodate, so the couple would need to leave their Hakodate hotel by roughly 06:30–06:45 to catch it.
- **This is the slowest option to reach Tokyo** (4h11–4h33 in transit alone, before even factoring the Hakodate→Shin-Hakodate-Hokuto local-train connection) and comparably priced to a flight — **not recommended** as the fastest/most reliable choice, though it's a reasonable fallback if flights are sold out, weather-cancelled, or if the couple simply wants the scenic Shinkansen experience once (in which case, the 07:38 departure is the only one that gets them to Tokyo with a genuine "mid-morning" feel by getting them there before lunch, and even then it's technically midday).

### Verdict

**Fly Hakodate (HKD) → Haneda (HND) on an early-morning flight.** This is the fastest and most reliable path to central Tokyo: ~1h20–1h30 flight + ~15–30 min Haneda→central Tokyo transfer, versus 4h11m+ for the Shinkansen alone (before local-train connection time) or the CTS option's need to backtrack from Hakodate to Sapporo first. An 08:00-ish HKD departure lands at HND by ~09:30, and the couple could be in central Tokyo (e.g. Shibuya/Shinjuku for costume shopping) by **10:00–10:30**, comfortably meeting the "arrive by mid-morning" goal with the afternoon free for Halloween costume shopping.

## 6. Hokkaido Rail Pass economics

**Current pricing** (per japan.travel, citing prices for tickets bought in Japan, subject to change): **5-Day Pass ¥20,000** (child ¥10,000); **7-Day Pass ¥26,000** (child ¥13,000); Flexible 4-Day Pass ¥9,500 ([japan.travel](https://www.japan.travel/en/my/enjoy-smart-trip-travel-passes/all-popular-sites-hokkaido-convenient-all-hokkaido-rail-pass/)). Note this differs slightly from the ¥22,000/¥28,000 figures referenced in the brief — treat the japan.travel figures above as the current sourced baseline and re-verify on JR Hokkaido's own official pass page immediately before purchase, since rail-pass pricing changes periodically (japan-guide.com confirms the pass covers unlimited JR trains **except the Hokkaido Shinkansen** and most JR buses, for 5/7/10 consecutive days) ([japan-guide.com](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e2361_01.html)).

**Important limitation:** the Hokkaido Rail Pass does **not** cover the Hokkaido Shinkansen — so if the couple ends up taking the Shin-Hakodate-Hokuto→Tokyo Shinkansen (Option c above), that leg is never covered by this pass regardless of purchase decision.

### Point-to-point fare sum for the recommended 7-night itinerary (Section 8 routing)

| Leg | Fare (one-way, per person) |
|---|---|
| CTS Airport → Sapporo (JR Rapid Airport) | ¥1,150 |
| Sapporo → Otaru (round trip, day visit) | ¥750 × 2 = ¥1,500 |
| Sapporo → Noboribetsu (JR Stn) via Hokuto | ¥4,070–4,890 |
| JR Noboribetsu Stn → Noboribetsu Onsen (bus) | ¥350 |
| Noboribetsu Onsen → JR Noboribetsu Stn (bus, return) | ¥350 |
| JR Noboribetsu Stn → Hakodate via Hokuto | ~¥7,000–8,000 (estimate; verify exact fare, this is a partial-route segment) |
| **Total (excl. final Hakodate→Tokyo leg, which is separate regardless)** | **~¥14,500–16,000 per person** |

**Recommendation: do NOT buy the Hokkaido Rail Pass for this itinerary.** Even the cheapest 5-Day Pass (¥20,000) costs **more** than the full point-to-point rail total of this specific 7-night route (~¥14,500–16,000), because the itinerary only involves **one long Hokuto leg each way** (Sapporo↔Noboribetsu↔Hakodate) plus a short Otaru round trip and airport transfer — it doesn't have enough total rail mileage to break even against the pass price. The math only favors the pass on itineraries with **multiple long-distance Hokuto/Shinkansen-adjacent legs per day** or **repeated Sapporo↔Hakodate round trips** — neither applies here. **Buy point-to-point tickets instead** (via the JR ticket machines / midori-no-madoguchi counters, or Klook/Navitime online reservation for peace of mind on the Hokuto legs).

## 7. Weather and packing, 24–31 October

| City | Avg high | Avg low | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sapporo | ~14–16°C (57–61°F), dropping toward **~13°C by month-end** | ~6–7°C (42–45°F), dropping toward **~5.8°C by month-end** | [weather-atlas.com](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/japan/sapporo-weather-october), [weather-and-climate.com](https://weather-and-climate.com/sapporo-October-averages) |
| Hakodate | ~15–17°C (58–63°F) at month start, cooling to **~13°C by month-end** | ~11–12°C (53°F) early month, cooling to **~6°C (42°F) by month-end** | [weatherspark.com](https://weatherspark.com/m/144080/10/Average-Weather-in-Hakodate-Japan) |

**First snow:** one source explicitly flags that Sapporo can see "Japan's earliest first snow" by the **end of October**, with average temperatures dropping rapidly from ~16°C to ~6°C across the month ([japanhighlights.com](https://www.japanhighlights.com/japan/hokkaido/weather-in-october)). This is a real (if not certain) possibility for the tail end of this trip — light snow or snow flurries in Sapporo by 30–31 Oct is not out of the question, though accumulating snow is unlikely this early.

**Rain:** October in Hokkaido is generally a drier month than Honshu's typhoon-prone Sept, but scattered rain days should be expected; no single authoritative daily-probability figure was found across sources, so plan for **layered, weather-flexible clothing** rather than betting on either dry or wet.

**Packing recommendations for a Madrid couple** (used to a Mediterranean climate, mild autumns):
- **Warm layers are essential** — this will feel notably colder than Madrid's late October (Madrid averages ~18–20°C/64–68°F highs in late Oct). Bring a proper insulated jacket, not just a light coat.
- Base layers (thermal top/leggings) for evenings, especially for outdoor onsen footbaths and ropeway rides at altitude/height where wind chill is sharper.
- A warm hat, scarf, and gloves — genuinely useful given the possibility of first snow / near-freezing lows by month-end.
- Waterproof or water-resistant shoes with decent grip — useful for Jigokudani's boardwalk (can be damp) and any rain days, and worth having in case of an early light snow.
- A packable rain shell/umbrella.
- Onsen-specific: any small tattoo-cover patches if they want the option at a public bath as a backup (see companion doc), though the plan should default to private baths regardless.

## 8. Recommended 7-night sequence, day by day

This is a refined version of **Route Option C** — it maximizes Sapporo's rich food/city-sight density (matching the couple's stated food-forward preference), keeps total transit exposure to 3 travel days out of 7, places the Noboribetsu onsen night for maximum relaxation mid-trip, and ends in Hakodate for the fastest possible Tokyo departure via HKD→HND.

| Date | Day | Plan | Transit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sat 24 Oct** | Arrival | Land NRT T2 09:30 → connect to NRT→CTS flight (target 14:00–16:00 departure) → land CTS ~15:45–17:45 → JR Rapid Airport to Sapporo (~35 min) → check in, evening: Ramen Yokocho or soup curry dinner in Susukino | Flight NRT–CTS (~1h35–1h45) + JR Rapid Airport (~35 min) |

| **Tue 27 Oct** | Sapporo → Noboribetsu | Morning: Odori Park, TV Tower, underground city browse → early afternoon Hokuto to Noboribetsu (JR Stn) + bus to onsen town → Jigokudani boardwalk + free Oyunumagawa footbath at golden hour → check into Bourou Noguchi or Takinoya (private in-room bath), relaxed onsen dinner | Sapporo→Noboribetsu (~1h10 Hokuto) + bus (~15 min) |

| **Sat 31 Oct AM** | Depart for Tokyo | Early flight HKD→HND (target ~08:00 departure) → land HND ~09:30 → monorail/Keikyu to central Tokyo → arrive central Tokyo ~10:00–10:30, afternoon free for Halloween costume shopping | Flight HKD–HND (~1h20–1h30) + Haneda transfer (~15–30 min) |

**Total point-to-point rail cost (excl. HKD–HND flight): ~¥14,500–16,000/person** — buy individual tickets, skip the Hokkaido Rail Pass (Section 6). **This sequence touches only 3 inter-city travel legs** (Sapporo→Noboribetsu, Noboribetsu→Hakodate, and the Otaru day-trip round-trip), leaving 4 full, unhurried city/onsen days — appropriate for a couple prioritizing food, atmosphere, and relaxation over checklist sightseeing.
