# Flight — Narita to Sapporo, 24 Oct 2026

## Decision: Peach from Narita Terminal 1

~8 flights a day. **Terminal 1 South Wing — a ten-minute free shuttle** from your T2 arrival.
**~¥10,500–15,000 pp**, plus **¥5,000–7,000 for two checked bags** (LCC base fares exclude them).
Target a **14:00–16:00** departure, landing New Chitose around 15:15–16:15.

**24 October is the last day of the summer timetable** — those flights run on the already-published schedule, so no changeover risk.

Then **JR Rapid Airport → Sapporo, 35 min, ¥1,150 pp — cash**, since it falls outside the pass window.

---

## Why not JAL, despite Iberia Plus

**JAL does not operate Narita → New Chitose.** Verified — their entire Sapporo service is from **Haneda**, about 20 flights a day.

As an **Iberia Plus** member, **only JAL metal earns you Avios**; Peach and Jetstar earn nothing in that programme. So earning requires a Haneda detour.

| | Total pp | Bags | Arrive Sapporo | Miles |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Peach, direct from Narita T1** | ~¥10,500–15,000 | ✗ +¥5,000–7,000 for two | ~15:15–16:15 | ✗ none |
| JAL via Haneda, Explorer Pass | ~¥14,600 *(¥11,000 + ¥3,600 bus)* | ✅ 2×23 kg included | ~16:15–16:45 | ⚠️ unverified |

The **JAL Japan Explorer Pass is ¥11,000 flat with two 23 kg bags included**, and it's open to anyone holding an international ticket on any airline — your Iberia booking reference qualifies. **So price is roughly a wash.**

**What the detour costs is ~90 minutes and a forfeit risk.** The Limousine Bus is 75–85 minutes plus road traffic, and no airline sells a through-ticket across a ground transfer between two airports — so the domestic leg is a separate ticket either way, and **if IB281 runs late that ticket is lost.** Two failure points instead of one, on a day you've already flown fourteen hours.

**And the payoff is small even if it works.** Haneda–New Chitose is only about **510 miles** — a few hundred Avios at full accrual, rounding error against a Madrid–Tokyo round trip. ⚠️ **Whether Explorer Pass fares earn Avios at all is unverified**; deeply discounted promotional fares often earn reduced or zero miles. Check the fare rules on jal.co.jp if you want to overrule this decision.

## Also considered

**ANA from Narita** — runs only **1–2 daily** on NRT–CTS and a departure in the window couldn't be confirmed; it may be seasonal. Not the reliable Narita option it first appeared to be.

**Jetstar Japan from Narita Terminal 3** (~15 min from T2) — comparable price, and it **earns JAL Mileage Bank miles directly** through a bilateral partnership. That would be the best answer if you held JMB rather than Iberia Plus, since it earns without any airport transfer. Jetstar does **not** credit to Iberia Plus.

## Return — 11 Nov

**IB282 departs Narita 14:10.** ⚠️ Confirm on your booking whether this is a single leg. Madrid is UTC+1 by then (DST ended 25 Oct) and a westbound Tokyo–Madrid leg runs 14–15 hours, so a direct lands around **20:00–21:00 the same evening** — **05:45 cannot be a direct arrival**, and would imply a connection with an overnight layover. Worth knowing where and how long before you're 30 hours into a journey.

**Either way you don't gain a night in Japan** — you still leave Narita on the 11th, so 24 Oct to 10 Nov is 18 nights.

**Airport transfer:** the plan assumed **N'EX at ¥3,070 pp**. With two large suitcases the **Access Narita bus at ~¥1,300 pp** is both cheaper and easier — swap it when finalising.
