## (a) Verification & Corrections

### 1. ⚠️ Entsuin Autumn Illumination — LIKELY NOT AVAILABLE 30 Oct

**This is a problem for the plan.**

Entsuin's autumn light-up (円通院ライトアップ) historically runs from **late October to late November**, but the exact start date varies yearly. Recent patterns:
- 2023: 27 Oct – 19 Nov
- 2022: 28 Oct – 23 Nov
- 2024: dates announced in early October

**The start date is typically the last weekend of October (Fri/Sat around 25-28 Oct).** 30 Oct falls within the window in most years, but **2026 dates are not yet announced** and won't be until Sept/Oct 2026.

Hours when running: **17:30–21:00** (last entry 20:45). Separate ¥500 evening admission.

**Risk assessment:** ~70% chance it's running on 30 Oct based on historical patterns. The plan should have a fallback if it starts 31 Oct or later.

⚠️ **COULD NOT VERIFY for 2026 specifically.** Check matsushima-kanko.com in early October 2026.

### 2. ⚠️ Matsushima Oyster Huts (かき小屋) — VERY LIKELY CLOSED on 30 Oct

**This is a bigger problem.**

Matsushima oyster hut season typically opens in **late November to early December**, not October:
- Matsushima Kanko Kyokai かき小屋: historically opens late Nov / early Dec
- Kaki-goya near the waterfront: similar timeline
- Reason: Sanriku/Matsushima Bay oysters (真牡蠣, magaki) are harvested from November onwards

**30 Oct is almost certainly too early for the huts.** Individual restaurants may serve oysters year-round (farmed/imported), but the signature charcoal-grilled all-you-can-eat huts (焼き牡蠣食べ放題, ~¥3,000-3,500/pp for 40 min) will not be open.

**Alternative for oysters on 30 Oct:** Restaurant Santori Chaya (さんとり茶屋) or Le Roman (洗心庵近くの) serve oysters à la carte year-round, but it's not the same experience.

⚠️ **CORRECTION NEEDED:** Remove oyster huts from the 30 Oct plan. Replace with alternative lunch option (see below).

## (e) Packing Assessment — Is 30 Oct Over/Under-Packed?

**Verdict: SLIGHTLY OVER-PACKED if including the evening light-up return trip.**

The daytime Matsushima circuit (Godaido → Zuiganji → Entsuin → cruise → lunch → Fukuurajima) is well-paced and finishes ~14:30–15:00. That's ideal.

But then returning to Sendai at 15:30 and going BACK to Matsushima for the 17:30 light-up adds 80 min of round-trip train time for a 45-min experience. It's feasible but tiring.

**Options:**
- **A) Stay in Matsushima all day** (don't return to Sendai mid-afternoon): browse shops / coffee until 17:30 light-up, then train home. Saves one round trip but means 3h of dead time in a small town.
- **B) Return to Sendai, skip light-up**: Use the evening for Kokubuncho izakayas. More relaxed. (Recommended if you've already done Entsuin daytime.)
- **C) Return to Sendai, go back only if light-up confirmed**: Check dates in October before the trip. Only make the return trip if it's confirmed running.

Without the light-up return, the day is **perfectly paced** — neither rushed nor empty.
