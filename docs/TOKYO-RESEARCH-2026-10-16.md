# Tokyo pool expansion research · 16 October 2026

The existing pool contained 14 outings and one evening: Akihabara, Shibuya shopping, Costco Kawasaki, Yokohama, Kawagoe, Kawaguchiko, Deep Fukagawa, Asakusa/Ueno, Shinjuku, Odaiba, Tsukiji/Ginza, Koenji, teamLab Planets, Hakone/Gora, and the Monzen-Nakacho izakaya evening. The expanded pool adds nine Tokyo outings and three evenings; dates remain unassigned.

## What was added

| Option | Why it adds something distinct | Main constraint checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yanesen old-town lanes + Nezu Shrine | Historic residential lanes, cemetery edge, shrine approach and small shops | The walk is mostly outdoors; shop hours vary. Yanaka Beer Hall is listed closed Mondays; Yanaka SUGIURA is listed closed Mondays and Tuesdays. Check both meal options before assigning a Monday/Tuesday. |
| Roppongi art triangle + Midtown | A weather-resilient choice centered on one art exhibition | Choose one museum only and check the date-specific exhibition calendar, closure day and admission conditions. The National Art Center is closed Tue 10 Nov and open Tue 3 Nov with a Wed 4 Nov closure; Mori is an alternative only if its live program is suitable. |
| Kichijoji + Inokashira Park | Local shopping streets paired with a major west-Tokyo park | Outdoor-led; Ghibli Museum is intentionally excluded because it requires its own advance ticket. |
| Ryogoku + Edo-Tokyo Museum | Indoor city-history visit with a sumo-quarter walk and chanko meal | The museum reopened in March 2026 and is closed Mondays; Mon 9 Nov is recorded as unavailable. No tournament or practice access is assumed. |
| Kagurazaka lanes + shrines | A former geisha quarter with steep lanes, shrines and local dining | Cobbled slopes can be slippery in rain. Kagurazaka Kurosu is a high-priced lunch fallback; check its menu and reservation conditions. |
| Ikebukuro Sunshine City | Concentrated indoor aquarium, observatory, and shopping option | The aquarium may restrict entry on busy days; its current FAQ recommends a timed-entry web voucher in those cases. Added a recommended planner booking action. |
| Shimokitazawa curry + small shops | Independent-shop neighborhood with a curry/seafood lunch choice | Shops open at different times; start the browse late morning and confirm restaurant opening hours. |
| Imperial Palace East Gardens + Tokyo Station | Edo Castle grounds and station architecture with nearby indoor shopping | Check the Imperial Household Agency’s closure calendar; palace interior access is not included. |
| Nezu Museum + Aoyama | Japanese/East Asian art and a garden with galleries as a rain option | The museum’s official 24 Oct–23 Nov 2026 exhibition calendar confirms closure on Mon 9 Nov; that date is recorded in `unavailableDates`. Exhibition tickets are timed-entry online, with same-day tickets also listed, so no advance booking is represented as mandatory. |
| Kagurazaka backstreets at dusk | A local evening walk and Japanese dinner | Sloped cobbled streets need care in rain; confirm restaurant hours and reservation availability. |
| Ryogoku chanko dinner | A nearby sumo-themed meal with minimal sightseeing overhead | Restaurant schedules can vary by season; confirm current hours and availability. |
| Kichijoji yakitori evening | A distinct west-side dinner to pair with the park route | Longer rail ride; check a suitable return train and dinner hours. |

All route coordinates are approximate map pins for the named destination or station area. Transit blocks are planning allowances from Comfort Hotel Tokyo Kiyosumi Shirakawa, not live journey-time or November timetable promises. Named restaurants are suggestions, not reservations. Venue hours, queues, prices, tickets, and November 2026 service should be checked directly before assigning a date. No seasonal festival or foliage claim is attached to the pool.

## Planner metadata

`tokyoPoolActivities` remains the public export. `TokyoPoolActivity` now supports `weatherFit`, `themes`, `planningCautions`, and `unavailableDates`; every existing and added option has weather and theme metadata. These weather labels describe the main visit type, not the forecast. `tokyoMoreActivities` uses a type-only import from `tokyoPool.ts` to avoid a runtime cycle. The Sunshine Aquarium's conditional timed-entry recommendation is surfaced as a `recommended` `bookingAction`, so it appears in planner bookings only when that option is assigned.
