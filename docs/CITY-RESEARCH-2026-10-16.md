## Route fit and limits

- **Sapporo:** alternatives are for 25–26 October. The museum can replace part of the Hokkaido University campus walk; Factory and Hokkaido Shrine are relaxed-day swaps. These do not add a stop to the 24 October arrival evening.
- **Hakodate:** alternatives are for 28 October, the full city day. Northern Peoples Museum can replace part of the hill walk if open; the Museum of Art is a rain-friendly Goryokaku-area swap; Rokkatei is a café break by the fort.
- **Sendai:** only the station-adjacent AER terrace and station-area zunda snack are listed for 29 October. This is an arrival day, so the terrace is only a brief swap for the planned downtown stroll after bag drop. There are no Sendai sightseeing suggestions on the 30 October Matsushima excursion or the 31 October departure day. The existing Rikyu backup is not duplicated.
- **Matsushima:** alternatives are for 30 October. Oshima is a nearby short walk if the cruise is skipped; Kanrantei is a nearby tea/view stop. Kaki no Ya opened in September 2026; it currently says it does not take reservations. Seafood Restaurant Tamaya is a named lunch replacement beside Godaido. Check service and menu on the day. The existing Matsushima Fish Market backup is not duplicated.
- **Nikko:** alternatives are for 3 November only. Tamozawa is an indoor heritage swap near the shrine corridor; its official schedule says it opens daily during 15 October–14 November despite the normal Tuesday closure. Meiji-no-Yakata is a lunch alternative; confirm Culture Day service and queues.
- **Kamakura:** alternatives are for 5 November. Engakuji is one station north from Kamakura and only makes sense if shortening the Hase/central route. Magokoro is in Hase and the Kanagawa tourism guide says vegetarians should contact the venue in advance; verify the precise menu and dietary handling directly.
- **Noboribetsu:** no alternatives added. The 27 October stop already has a narrow luggage, valley, lunch and rail plan; another outing, bath or bus excursion would undermine the return buffer. The current route includes its named lunch and a short town walk.

No Mt Hakodate Ropeway alternative is suggested. The operator’s [official maintenance notice](https://334.co.jp/news/2026/0406-22668/) lists a 20 October–8 November 2026 closure; the summit bus is not treated as a dependable substitute.
