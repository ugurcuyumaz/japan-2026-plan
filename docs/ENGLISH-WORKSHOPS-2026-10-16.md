## Closest useful cluster: Nihonbashi

[Omotenashi Nihonbashi's operator page](https://nihonbashi-info.jp/omotenashi/workshop.html) explicitly says its workshops run in English; partner workshops use an accompanying concierge/interpreter.

| Option | Published duration and price | Practical details |
|---|---|---|
| Origami with washi paper | 30 min ¥1,500/person, or 60 min ¥2,500/person | Daily 11:30–18:00; 1–5 participants. Fold and take home designs. Advance or same-day reservation. |
| Ozu Washi paper-making via concierge | About 60 min; ¥9,000/group, up to 4 people | Tue–Sat 11:30/14:00/15:00. English explanations throughout; take home paper. Advance request. For two, group total remains ¥9,000. |
| Nijiyura tenugui dyeing via concierge | About 90 min; ¥10,600/person | Up to 2 people; schedule by inquiry. English guidance from paste application through drying. Take home towel. Advance request. |

Information center: COREDO Muromachi 1 B1, 2-2-1 Nihonbashi Muromachi; direct station access from Mitsukoshimae. Partner meeting points must be confirmed. Washi fits the published weekday pattern on **4, 6, 7 and 10 November**, not 1, 8 or 9. These are candidate dates, not seats.

[Participation terms](https://nihonbashi-info.jp/omotenashi/attention.html): generally no refund after payment; partner workshops may cancel when over 15 minutes late. Video recording is prohibited. [Partner-workshop request form](https://form.jotform.com/262232423112037) asks for preferred and alternate dates; submitting it does not prove confirmation. Use the origami reservation link on the operator page for origami.

**Planner judgment:** Nihonbashi is a convenient east/central Tokyo choice for the Kiyosumi base. Route via Mitsukoshimae; check live directions, rather than publishing an exact door-to-door time. Origami is the cheapest and easiest short option. Paper-making is the best two-person value among the larger craft experiences. Tenugui is distinct from the old indigo idea, but choose one textile class rather than both by default.

## 4. Sokichi · kiriko glass engraving, Asakusa

- **English evidence:** the [operator FAQ](https://en.sokichi-workshop.com/q-a) explicitly offers workshops in Japanese, English and Chinese. Select English when requesting the session; the separate continuing skill-development course is Japanese.
- **Published class:** [90-minute course](https://en.sokichi-workshop.com/90min), adult ¥3,630/person for a clear base glass; colored/shaped glass adds ¥1,100–4,510. Listed weekday starts 10:15/13:00/15:00; weekends have additional starts, with a surcharge for 17:00. Do not imply these starts have vacancies.
- **Location:** [official access](https://en.sokichi-workshop.com/access): 2-1-14 Kaminarimon, Taito; 30-second walk from Ginza-line Asakusa Exit 4 or three minutes from Asakusa-line Exit A3.
- **Prep:** [operator overview](https://en.sokichi-workshop.com/) lists hair ties/eyeglasses if needed. FAQ says upstairs with no elevator and offers shipping by quote. Protect the finished glass in luggage.
- **Booking caveat:** overview says cash only, while the [linked booking form](https://booking.sokichi-workshop.com/?course=lecture&lang=en) describes availability confirmation followed by payment. Confirm payment instructions for the selected course; do not assume a submitted form is a reservation.

**Planner judgment:** good compact Asakusa anchor, paired with nearby sightseeing. Replace the old unnamed kiriko placeholder only through an explicit traveler choice, without claiming its old price or studio was confirmed.

## 5. mon cadeau · modern kintsugi, Shibuya

[Official site](https://www.moncadeau.jp/) explicitly states that modern classes are taught in English. The standard class is two hours and ¥19,980/person (¥39,960 for two), with materials, matcha/sweets and a protective wooden box. It uses modern repair materials and gold-colored powder; do not describe it as an entire traditional urushi restoration. Location: room 401, Dogenzaka Plaza Nishinaya Building, 1-18-8 Dogenzaka, Shibuya. Wear clothes suitable for glue/paint. Traditional members' classes are in Japanese and unsuitable for this short trip.

[Classes and cancellation terms](https://www.moncadeau.jp/ja/book): the modern class is English; cancellations incur a 10% fee, and within seven days are non-refundable. The 2.5-hour tea-ceremony variant is ¥24,980/person and requires a request for two or more, subject to date confirmation. No November session availability verified. Do not promise food-safe use or a curing time: ask the operator for care instructions.

**Planner judgment:** worthwhile premium alternative on a Shibuya day; do not add it to an already full west-Tokyo shopping route without shortening that route. Standard class is sufficient; keep the tea option inside its details, not as a duplicate pool card.

## 6. Cooking Sun Tokyo · sushi or Japanese home cooking

[Operator home page](https://www.cooking-sun.com/) explicitly says lessons are in English. The [Tokyo course page](https://www.cooking-sun.com/tokyo-cooking-class/) lists daily three-hour classes: Wagyu + seven dishes 09:30–12:30; sushi 13:30–16:30. For two or three adults booking together, the rate is ¥10,000/person (¥20,000 for two); the solo rate is ¥10,500. Sushi includes several styles, egg and soup, using pre-sliced fish; no sashimi-cutting lesson. Discuss dietary substitutions before booking.

Studio: Maya Shinanomachi II room 314, 18-39 Shinanomachi, Shinjuku; course page says 7–8 minutes from JR Shinanomachi or 10–13 from Yotsuya-sanchome. Use the right-hand entrance/intercom 314 and save its detailed directions. Cancellation refund ladder: more than seven days 100%, 2–6 days 80%, one day 50%, day-of/no-show none. The stated ladder leaves the exact seven-day boundary unclear: check checkout terms. November vacancies unverified.

**Planner judgment:** choose one menu. Treat the class meal as the meal anchor instead of inserting an extra full lunch. It is a cross-city workshop from Kiyosumi, not a near-hotel option. Useful with a shortened Shinjuku/Aoyama day.

## 7. Taiko-Lab · English private taiko lesson

[Operator's visitor program](https://www.taikolab.com/world/) explicitly teaches in easy English and welcomes couples/individuals. A 60-minute private program includes drumming instruction, instruments/sticks and an instructor performance. Studios: Aoyama, Daiwa Aoyama B1, 3-1-30 Jingumae; Akihabara, 18-1 Kanda Neribeicho. Confirm which studio is actually booked.

[GO TOKYO's official Aoyama listing](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/workshop/sd033/index.html), updated January 2026, publishes a ¥6,160 taiko-experience reference and four-minute walk from Gaienmae Exit 3. It does **not establish that ¥6,160 is the final per-person price for a two-person private English lesson**. Display “Ask for a two-person quote” as the price, not ¥12,320 for two. Operator inquiry is needed for date, quote, meeting point and preparation/cancellation instructions. No dated vacancies checked.

**Planner judgment:** the most active option, and a change from seated crafts. Prefer the Akihabara studio if offered for a date that already focuses on east Tokyo; use its confirmed address in directions. Do not confuse a membership trial lesson with the visitor private program.
