## 1. Ichibanboshi — local izakaya / yoshoku

- **Address:** 1-4-18 Shirakawa, Furuya Building 1-B, Koto-ku. Japanese: 下町食楽酒房 いちばん星.
- **Why:** an easy local evening with pork sauté, garlic shrimp and seasonal dishes; also serves proper meals rather than requiring a drinking-focused night.
- **Hours:** lunch 11:30–14:00; dinner 17:00–23:00, subject to change. Dinner closed **Monday and Tuesday**; lunch closed Monday and second/fourth Sunday.
- **Trip fit:** dinner candidates **4, 5, 6, 7 or 8 November**. Do not suggest it as dinner on 3, 9 or 10 November. No holiday exception verified. Booking requirement unknown; ask before relying on it.
- **English:** English tourism article exists; this is **not evidence of an English menu or English-speaking service**. Both unknown.
- **Source:** [Koto ward official shop introduction, July 2026 interview](https://kotomise.jp/shops/下町食楽酒房いちばん星/変わらない味、変わっていく街受け継がれる「い/). Use the current interview rather than the site's 2015 article.
- **Map:** `[35.6810414, 139.8010309]`, from the ward listing's explicit map link. About **200 m straight-line** from the corrected Comfort address.

## 3. CLANN BY THE RIVER — riverside grill and craft beer

- **Address:** 1-1-7 Kiyosumi, LYURO Tokyo Kiyosumi 2F, Koto-ku.
- **Why:** pulled pork / grill dishes, tapas and craft beer for a relaxed evening by the Sumida River. This is a restaurant; customers do not cook their own barbecue.
- **Hours:** normal dinner 17:00–22:00, food last order 21:00. **Cashless payment only.** Reservations offered for dinner; terrace seating cannot be reserved.
- **Critical trip exception:** **9 November 2026 is breakfast-only, 07:00–10:00 (last order 09:30), due to maintenance. No lunch or dinner that day.** Other candidate dinners: 3–8 and 10 November, subject to current availability.
- **English:** official English booking flow and English course descriptions; spoken service not confirmed. Do not label the entire physical menu English based on this alone.
- **Sources:** [official restaurant](https://clannbytheriver.jp/), [host hotel's meal information](https://www.thesharehotels.com/lyuro/restaurant/), [dated maintenance notice](https://www.thesharehotels.com/lyuro/news/1025/), [operator-linked booking terms](https://www.tablecheck.com/en/shops/pitmans-kiyosumi/reserve).

- **Hours:** 17:00–23:30, food last order 22:30 / drinks 23:00; no regular closed day listed.
