# Japan planner CX review — 15 October 2026

## What the planner needs to do

Help two travelers prepare for the booked Japan trip, quickly find the right day while traveling, understand what is fixed versus optional, and move from a place to directions or a reservation without losing context. All hotels and flights are confirmed; other arrangements remain open. The trip facts and researched alternatives are retained.

## Map behavior

Selecting a base reveals its dates, booked hotels and itinerary days. Selecting a place reveals its research and actions. Selecting a group reveals all of its stops, including exact coordinate duplicates. The overview initially fits the whole route; day maps fit the local visit while retaining long-distance endpoints in the place list. A dashed line means stop order, not a verified walking, driving or rail path. Google Maps directions remain the navigation action.

The vector renderer is loaded separately from the main planner. Tile and style availability depends on the external map service; itinerary content and place links remain usable without it.
