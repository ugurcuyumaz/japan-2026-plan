## Detailed Property Analysis

### 1. Noboribetsu Sekisuitei (登別石水亭) — BEST BUDGET OPTION WITH KASHIKIRI

- **Private bath:** CONFIRMED — kashikiri bath available at **¥3,300 for 50 minutes** ([source: official FAQ](https://www.sekisuitei.com/en/faq_cat/spa/))
- **How to book:** Email or phone after room booking is confirmed
- **Room rate:** ¥15,000–30,000 for 2 adults depending on plan (Kayak shows "from $108" ≈ ¥16,500; [mai-ko.com](https://mai-ko.com/travel/japan/ryokan/noboribetsu-sekisui-tei/) says "15,000 to 30,000 JPY per night")
- **Meal plans:** Half-board is standard via official booking. Jalan/Rakuten may offer breakfast-only plans. "Ichiba meshi" seafood buffet is the standard dinner option.
- **Tattoo policy:** NOT explicitly stated on English site. The kashikiri bath is PRIVATE so tattoos are irrelevant for that 50-min slot.
- **Shuttle:** ¥1,000/person each way from JR Noboribetsu station ([source](https://www.sekisuitei.com/en/category/info/))
- **Total estimated cost:** Room ~¥20,000 + kashikiri ¥3,300 = **~¥23,300 (€137) for the cheapest viable combination**
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM-HIGH on kashikiri price (official site, though 2018 post — may have risen). Room rate is indicative range from aggregator sites, not a live Oct 2026 quote.

### 2. Hotel Mahoroba (ホテルまほろば) — TATTOO-FRIENDLY + CHEAPEST STANDARD ROOMS

- **Private bath:** Has rooms with open-air bath (premium category). Also has 31+ communal baths and reportedly tattoos are ALLOWED in public areas.
- **Tattoo policy:** **CONFIRMED TATTOO-FRIENDLY** — [japanese-onsen.com](https://japanese-onsen.com/ryokans/hotel-mahoroba/) states: "Guests with tattoos are welcome to use the public hot springs at Hotel Mahoroba."
- **Room rate:** From ¥18,000–26,000 (2p) for standard rooms with meals ([ohotel.com](https://mahorobanobori.ohotel.com/) "from $113"; [Hotels.com UK](https://uk.hotels.com/ho457447/hotel-mahoroba-noboribetsu-japan/) shows "£103" ≈ ¥21,000). Breakfast-only plan available on [Rakuten](https://travel.rakuten.com/usa/en-us/hotel_info_item/cnt_japan/sub_hokkaido/cty_noboribetsu_city/10123456849967/).
- **Key insight:** Because tattoos are ALLOWED in the public baths, you don't actually need a private bath here! The communal experience is available to you.
- **For in-room private bath room:** Expect ¥35,000–50,000+ (premium tier)
- **Shuttle:** Free shuttle likely (hotel is 15-min bus ride from JR station; shuttle unconfirmed on English site)
- **Total estimated cost:** **~¥18,000–26,000 (€106–153) standard room with tattoos welcome in public baths**
- **Confidence:** HIGH on tattoo policy. MEDIUM on exact room rate for Oct 26 2026 (based on recent aggregator pricing, not a live booking).

- **Private bath:** Has **rooms with Japanese cypress bath** (檜風呂) confirmed on [official EN site](https://www.nobogura.co.jp/en/): "Suites with a cypress bath and guest rooms with an open-air bath are available"
- **Room rate:** Standard rooms from ~¥20,000–35,000 (2p HB). Cypress-bath rooms likely ¥35,000–55,000. [Kayak.ca](https://www.ca.kayak.com/Noboribetsu-Hotels-Noboribetsu-Grand-Hotel.415407.ksp) shows "C$189" (≈ ¥20,000) for standard. [Jalan](https://www.jalan.net/en/japan_hotels_ryokan/Ryokan/Hokkaido_Ryokan/Toya_Noboribetsu_Tomakomai_Ryokan/Noboribetsu_Muroran_Shiraoi_Ryokan/noboribetsu_grand_hotel/) shows "USD 206" for some plan.
- **Tattoo policy:** Not explicitly stated. No private kashikiri — only the in-room cypress bath option.
- **Note:** Under renovation June–July 2026, completing "autumn" per [nobogura.co.jp](https://www.nobogura.co.jp/). October should be post-renovation.
- **Total estimated cost for cypress-bath room:** **~¥35,000–55,000 (€206–323)**
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM — price range inferred from similar properties and the "USD 206" Jalan reference.

### 4. Dai-ichi Takimotokan (第一滝本館)

- **Private bath:** Has rooms with private rotenburo (highest room tiers). NO kashikiri for day visitors ([official site](https://takimotokan.co.jp/en/day_spa/) explicitly says "The private room does not include a private bath").
- **Room rate:** No-meal plan from ~$213 for 2 adults ([Rakuten](https://travel.rakuten.com/usa/en-us/hotel_info_item/10123456795913) shows "$212.94"). Private-bath rooms significantly more expensive (~¥40,000–60,000 HB).
- **Tattoo policy:** "More lenient in recent years" per [japanactivity.com](https://japanactivity.com/where-to-stay-in-noboribetsu) — "verify before booking if this applies to you"
- **No-meal plan:** CONFIRMED available ([Rakuten](https://travel.rakuten.com/usa/en-us/hotel_info_item/cnt_japan/sub_hokkaido/cty_noboribetsu_city/10123456795913/?features=18123456791830): "Plan without meals to enjoy hot springs")

- **Confidence:** HIGH on no-meal plan existence. MEDIUM on private-bath room pricing (inferred range).

## CHEAPEST VIABLE OPTIONS — Final Ranking

### 🏆 Option A: Hotel Mahoroba (standard room) — ~€106–153

**Why:** Tattoo-friendly public baths means you don't NEED a private bath. This is genuinely the cheapest way to get a full onsen experience in Noboribetsu as a tattooed person. Breakfast-only plans exist on Rakuten (further savings). Massive 31-bath complex.

**Risk:** Verify tattoo policy hasn't changed for 2026. The source is credible (japanese-onsen.com) but policies can reverse.

### 🥈 Option B: Noboribetsu Sekisuitei (standard room + kashikiri) — ~€137–195

**Why:** Confirmed kashikiri at ¥3,300/50min. Budget room + private bath session. Noguchi Kanko Group (reputable chain). 8th-floor bath with nature views.

**Risk:** Kashikiri price may have risen since 2018. Only 50 minutes. Need to book bath separately after room confirmation. Shuttle costs ¥2,000 return for 2.

### 🥉 Option C: Noboribetsu Grand Hotel (cypress bath room) — ~€206–323

**Why:** In-room private bath, post-renovation (fresh). Historic property. Right next to bus terminal.

**Risk:** Cypress-bath room likely at the UPPER end of this range (€250+). Standard rooms have NO private bath option.

## Toyako Onsen Alternative (JR Hokuto — Toya station)

Toyako is one stop closer to Hakodate on the Hokuto line (Toya station, then bus/taxi to lakeside). The **Noguchi Kanko Group** operates multiple properties there:

- **Toya Kohantei** — Lake Toya lakeside. Kashikiri likely available (same group as Sekisuitei). Expected ¥15,000–30,000 range.
- **Toya Gokirakutei** — More casual option, same group.
- **The Lake View TOYA Nonokaze Resort** — Superior tier, likely has in-room baths.

Toyako is generally slightly cheaper than Noboribetsu and less touristy. Lake views instead of volcanic views. However:
- Less famous water quality (sodium chloride vs Noboribetsu's 9 mineral types)
- Bus connection from Toya station is less frequent than Noboribetsu
- Property availability for late October unconfirmed

**Verdict:** Worth checking Toya Kohantei pricing as a backup, but Noboribetsu has better onsen variety and more confirmed options.

## Key Recommendations

1. **BOOK MAHOROBA FIRST** if you're comfortable with tattoo-friendly communal baths (enormous 31-bath complex, multiple water types, very cheap)
2. **BOOK SEKISUITEI** if you want a GUARANTEED private soak (kashikiri confirmed, ¥3,300 add-on is trivial)
3. **Contact both directly** to confirm Oct 26 2026 availability and ask for breakfast-only/room-only rates
4. **Skip Takinoya** — at ¥75,000 HB it's 3-4x more expensive with no proportionate benefit for your needs
