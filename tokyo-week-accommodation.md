# Tokyo Week Accommodation — Research

## Verdict

**Apart-hotel wins for this trip.** Specifically **Tokyu Stay Monzen-Nakacho** (~¥84,000–126,000 / €525–788 for 7 nights): in-room washer-dryer, kitchenette, staffed reception for takkyubin + luggage storage, hotel-licensed (zero minpaku legal risk), 3 min to station, surrounded by Tokyo's best non-touristy izakaya scene. If budget is paramount, a **licensed Airbnb in a permissive ward** (Koto, Sumida, Taito) saves ~¥20,000–40,000 over 7 nights but loses takkyubin receipt and arrival-day luggage handling — manageable trade-offs only if the listing explicitly accepts deliveries.

## Part 1: Legal Situation — Minpaku Law & Tokyo Restrictions

### 1.1 National Framework (住宅宿泊事業法, enacted 2018-06-15)

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Annual cap** | 180 nights/year per property (noon Apr 1 – noon Apr 1) [1][2] |
| **Registration** | Mandatory notification to ward governor; issued **M + 9 digits** (e.g., M123456789) [1][6] |
| **Display requirement** | Number must appear on listing page AND physical sign at property [1] |
| **Guest registry** | Name, nationality, passport number (non-residents); retain 3 years [1] |
| **Host-absent** | Must appoint registered administrator if host is not on-site [1] |
| **Platforms** | Legally required to delist unregistered properties (enforced since Jun 2018 crackdown) [6] |

### 1.2 The Three Licence Types (Critical Distinction)

| Type | Legal basis | Day limit | Weekday restrictions | Number format |
|------|------------|-----------|---------------------|---------------|
| **Minpaku** | 住宅宿泊事業法 | 180 days (or less per ward) | YES — varies by ward | M + 9 digits |
| **Hotel/ryokan** (簡易宿所) | 旅館業法 | **None — 365 days** | **None** | Ward-issued (not M-prefix) |
| **Tokku** (特区民泊) | National Strategic Special Zones | None (min 2-night stay) | **None** | Zone-specific |

**Key takeaway:** A property with a **旅館業法 licence** (non-M-prefix number, listed as "Licensed Vacation Rental" on Airbnb) has ZERO day restrictions. All apart-hotel chains operate under this licence. [10][11]

### 1.3 Tokyo Ward-Level Restrictions

⚠️ **June 2026 regulatory shift:** The JTA formally empowered wards to ban minpaku entirely in residential zones ("zero-day regulation" / ゼロ日規制) [4][5][12]. Several wards are actively tightening.

| Ward | Restriction (residential zones) | Impact on Mon–Mon weekday stay |
|------|--------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| **Shinjuku** | ⛔ Banned Mon noon – Fri noon [4][7][14] | 🔴 ILLEGAL for weeknight stays |
| **Shibuya** | ⛔ Ordinance revised Jun 2026, zone-based restrictions [4] | 🔴 LIKELY PROBLEMATIC |
| **Minato** | ⚠️ Host-absent restricted in residential/educational zones [4][15] | 🟡 RISKY |
| **Chiyoda** | ⚠️ Jul 2026 ban near schools unless owner-resident [7][8] | 🟡 RISKY |
| **Toshima** | ⚠️ Cap reduced to 120 days/year [7][8] | 🟡 MODERATE RISK (quota exhaustion) |
| **Nakano** | ⚠️ National framework but "likely to tighten" [4] | 🟡 MONITOR |
| **Taito** | National framework only [4] | 🟢 LOWER RISK |
| **Sumida** | National framework only [4] | 🟢 LOWER RISK |
| **Chuo** | National framework only [4] | 🟢 LOWER RISK |
| **Koto** (Monzen-Nakacho, Kinshicho) | National framework only [4] | 🟢 LOWER RISK |
| **Ota** | Tokku zone — year-round, min 2-night stay [16] | 🟢 PERMISSIVE |

**Shinjuku detail:** "School holiday" exception covers extended break periods (spring/summer/winter), NOT individual national holidays like Culture Day (Nov 3). A Nov 4–10 stay in a Shinjuku residential-zone minpaku would be illegal Mon–Fri. ⚠️ VERIFY directly with ward.

### 1.4 Enforcement Reality (2025–2026)

- Registered properties grew to 40,000+ nationally by mid-2026 [7][8]
- Shinjuku issued **first cessation order** Dec 2025 [7]; conducting active weekday inspections
- Shinjuku alone: 1,334 complaints in FY2025 [8]
- Risk to guests: mid-stay shutdown, Airbnb cancellation, no legal recourse
- The 2018 crackdown cancelled thousands of bookings 10 days before check-in [6]

### 1.5 What Travellers Should Look For

| ✅ Safe indicators | 🔴 Red flags |
|-------------------|-------------|
| Non-M-prefix licence number (旅館業法) | No registration number displayed |
| "Licensed Vacation Rental" badge on Airbnb | Exact address withheld until payment |
| Apart-hotel chain (Tokyu Stay, MIMARU, etc.) | Host asks you to claim you're "a friend" |
| StayJapan listing (all verified legal) [18] | Significantly below-market price |
| Superhost with verified reviews | Check-in designed to avoid building staff |

### 1.6 Safer Platforms & Categories

| Platform | Legality assurance | Notes |
|----------|-------------------|-------|
| **StayJapan** | ✅ HIGH — "first website to offer legal short-term rentals in Japan" [18] | Rural/traditional focus; less urban Tokyo inventory |

## Part 3: Things That Actually Matter for a Week

### 3.1 Washing Machine & Drying

**The critical gap:** Most Japanese apartments have a washer but **no tumble dryer**. Options:

| Drying method | Time (November) | Sufficient for 7 nights? |
|---------------|-----------------|--------------------------|
| **Washer-dryer combo** (Tokyu Stay, MIMARU SUITES, Oakwood) | 2–3h full cycle | ✅ Best — wash+dry in one go |
| **浴室乾燥機 (bathroom ventilation dryer)** — most Airbnbs | 3–4h per 2–3 kg load [13-src] | ✅ OK if run daily after evening bath |
| Indoor hanging + dehumidifier | 6–12h | ⚠️ Slow; fine for light items only |

**Apart-hotel chains with guaranteed in-room washer-dryer:**
- Tokyu Stay (ALL rooms) ✅
- MIMARU SUITES (Asakusa, Nihombashi) ✅ — but NOT standard MIMARU (coin laundry only)
- Oakwood (ALL rooms) ✅
- MONday Apart Premium (select rooms) ✅

**Day 12 of 18:** Laundry is non-negotiable. The washer-dryer combo at Tokyu Stay means you can do a full wash-and-dry in 2–3 hours without hanging clothes. With bathroom dryer only (most Airbnbs), plan to run it daily — start after bath, clothes dry by morning.

### 3.2 Luggage on Arrival Day (4 Nov, midday)

| Strategy | Feasibility | Cost |
|----------|-------------|------|
| **Hotel/apart-hotel front desk** | ✅ Best — arrive, drop bags, explore until check-in | Free |
| **Airbnb early check-in** | ⚠️ Negotiate with host in advance; not guaranteed | Free or ¥1,000–3,000 |
| **ecbo cloak** (app-based storage at shops) | ✅ Good backup | ¥800/bag/day |
| **Station coin lockers** | ❌ Large lockers (86×34×65cm) won't fit 75L+ suitcases; often full by midday | ¥700–1,000/locker |
| **Send via takkyubin from Nikko** (morning Nov 3 → arrives Nov 4) | ✅ Excellent if planned ahead | ¥2,000–3,000/suitcase [17-src] |

**Recommended:** If apart-hotel, drop bags at reception. If Airbnb, send suitcases from Nikko via takkyubin on Nov 3 morning (next-day delivery) and carry only daypacks on the train.

### 3.3 Takkyubin (宅急便) Delivery — Decision-Critical

| Accommodation type | Can receive takkyubin? | Why |
|-------------------|----------------------|-----|
| Hotels / apart-hotels | ✅ Yes — address to "Guest Name, arriving [date]" | Staffed reception signs for delivery |
| Airbnb / vacation rentals | ⚠️ **MAJOR ISSUE** | No reception = no one to sign. Driver leaves attempted-delivery notice (不在票). Must request redelivery. Many listings explicitly say "no deliveries" [21-src][22-src] |

**Workaround for Airbnb:** Specify evening delivery time window (18:00–20:00) so you're present. Or ask host if their management company can accept. But this is unreliable.

**⚠️ This is the single strongest argument for an apart-hotel over an Airbnb for this trip:** You're arriving from Nikko with 2 large suitcases. Sending them ahead via takkyubin is the sensible move (¥4,000–6,000 for both cases, same-region next-day). An apart-hotel with reception accepts them trivially. An Airbnb cannot.

### 3.4 Self-Check-In vs Reception

| Type | Method | 24h support | Luggage handling |
|------|--------|-------------|------------------|
| Apart-hotel (Tokyu Stay, MIMARU, Oakwood) | Staffed reception or automated terminal | ✅ Yes | ✅ Full |
| Airbnb (managed) | Smart lock / key box, code via app | ⚠️ App messaging only | ❌ None |
| Airbnb (individual host) | Variable — key box, meet-and-greet | ⚠️ Response time varies | ❌ None |

Smart locks in Japan are generally reliable post-2018, but no fallback if code fails or building auto-lock (オートロック) prevents courier access.

### 3.5 Cleaning Fees & Value (7-Night Stay)

| Factor | Airbnb | Apart-hotel | Business hotel |
|--------|--------|-------------|----------------|
| Cleaning fee | ¥3,000–8,000 once (→ ¥430–1,140/night amortised) | None | None |
| Weekly discount | 10–20% off nightly rate | Rare/small | Rare |
| Mid-stay cleaning | Not included (self-service) | On request (sometimes extra) | Daily, free |
| Checkout duties | Some: run washer, take out sorted trash | None | None |

**Net effect for 7 nights:** The flat cleaning fee + weekly discount makes Airbnb 20–30% better value per night than a 2-night booking at the same property. A ¥6,000 cleaning fee on a ¥12,000/night apartment = only ¥857/night extra over 7 nights.

### 3.6 Arrival Logistics (4 Nov)

**Date correction:** Nov 3, 2026 = Tuesday (Culture Day, public holiday). Nov 4, 2026 = **Wednesday** (regular working day). [30-src][31-src]

**Route from Nikko:** Tobu Spacia X → Tobu Asakusa Station (~1h 50min, ¥2,800–4,000 reserved). Then metro to accommodation.

**With 2 large suitcases:** Elevator routing matters at transfer stations. Asakusa, Shinjuku, Ikebukuro all have good elevator access. Consider taxi from Asakusa to final destination (¥2,500–4,500 depending on location) — faster and easier than metro transfers with 4 bags.
