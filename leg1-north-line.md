# Leg 1 — North Line (LOCKED 2026-07-29)

**Status:** ✅ shape agreed by uyumaz · costs + timetables under verification
**Dates:** Sat 2026-10-24 → Sat 2026-10-31 (7 nights)
**Ends:** central Tokyo by late morning 31 Oct for Halloween

## Shape

`NRT ✈ Sapporo (2n) → Hakodate (2n) → Sendai (1n) → Nikko (1n) → Chuzenji (1n) → Tokyo`

One continuous southward line. No backtracking. Four bases, three long transit legs.

## Trade-offs

| Pro | Con |
|---|---|
| Zero backtracking — geography flows north→south into Tokyo | Sendai is a single overnight: dinner + a walk, **no Matsushima** |
| Nikko gets 2 nights → Toshogu **and** Lake Chuzenji **and** a private-bath night | Arrival day is a write-off (dinner only) |
| 3 private-bath onsen nights available (Yunokawa, Chuzenji, optional Sendai→Naruko swap) | Brisk pace: 4 bases in 7 nights |
| Ends adjacent to Tokyo → short hop on Halloween morning | Hakodate→Sendai is a ~3h30 travel day ⚠️ unverified |
| Hokkaido food core (Sapporo + Hakodate) retained | Otaru compressed to a day trip |

**Single best don't-miss: the Mt Moiwa night view** — ranked among Japan's top three, car-free,
not season-dependent, and caps the Susukino evening naturally.

**Deliberately cut:** Moerenuma Park (45–60 min round trip — a second-full-day pick), Maruyama
Zoo / Hokkaido Shrine (redundant with the campus walk), the art museums, Sapporo Dome and Escon
Field (the latter is 30 min outside the city in Kitahiroshima).

❌ **White Illumination is dark** — the 2026–27 season starts **20 Nov**, roughly a month after you
leave. Same for the German Christmas Market. No other festival falls in the window; the ginkgo
light-up is the only live seasonal event, and it's already in the plan.

**26 Oct breakfast before the Hokuto:** Nijo Market opens ~07:00, but the safer choice with a
morning train is **Sapporo Station's own food halls** (Esta / Paseo / Apia) — early opening,
directly connected to the platforms, no outdoor walk.

**Rain plan:** Chi-Ka-Ho / Pole Town / Aurora Town underground arcades run Sapporo Station to
Susukino fully covered. Beer Museum and T38 are indoor and unaffected. Only the ginkgo walk and
Moiwa need substituting — Shiroi Koibito Park covers the first, an extra Susukino hour the second.

**Food bookings you'd make yourself:** sushi omakase 1–3 days ahead via **Tabelog's English site**
(Sushi Shiro, Sushi Dokoro Rin, Sushi Zen all take direct online bookings, no phone call) or
**OMAKASE.in** (~¥390/seat fee, removes the language barrier). Sapporo Beer Garden for dinner is
worth booking. Soup curry, Ramen Yokocho and the markets are queue-and-order.

### Why both Nikko nights in town

The 31 Oct departure is the constraint. The first inbound Tobu limited express is **08:52** and there is
nothing earlier. Sleeping at Lake Chuzenji means gambling on a mountain bus down Irohazaka to make it;
sleeping in Nikko town means **walking to the station**. Cost of this choice: no lakeside private-bath
room at Hotel Hana-An, and Chuzenji becomes an up-and-down day trip on the 30th with Irohazaka traffic
risk in **both** directions.

### Why Noboribetsu, not Jozankei, for the Hokkaido onsen night

uyumaz suggested "the onsen area in Sapporo" — that's **Jozankei**, 60–75 min by Jotetsu bus
southwest of Sapporo Station. Structurally worse for this route:

| | Noboribetsu | Jozankei |
|---|---|---|
| On the Hakodate line? | ✅ Hokuto stops there — **zero backtrack** | ❌ dead-end valley; must return to Sapporo (+75 min) |
| Cost to the Sapporo day | none | ⚠️ **kills Susukino dinner + Mt Moiwa night view** — both evening-only, and Moiwa is the day's single best item |
| Sightseeing on site | Jigokudani hell valley, free Oyunuma footbath | pleasant gorge, less to do |
| Private in-room baths | Takinoya, Bourou Noguchi — both confirmed | available, less documented |

**The day-use bath problem disappears.** The A/B/C choice (Sekisuitei's 15:00-only slot vs. the
Mt Hakodate sunset) only existed because Noboribetsu was a *day stop*. As an overnight you get the
in-room bath as part of the ryokan stay, take Jigokudani unhurried, **and** still get the Mt
Hakodate sunset — on the 27th instead of the 26th. Only *day-use* kashikiri was scarce in
Noboribetsu; overnight private baths are plentiful.

### Does Hakodate survive on one night?

Yes, because you sleep **station-side**. Arrive early afternoon on the 27th → Motomachi,
Goryokaku, Mt Hakodate at sunset. Next morning the **squid market is downstairs**: open 06:30,
you leave ~09:40 for the local to Shin-Hakodate-Hokuto. Tight but real — and it only works
because Yunokawa (a 20-min bus out of town) is gone.

Cost is roughly neutral: you swap a Yunokawa private-bath room (¥38,800–64,600) for a Noboribetsu
ryokan of similar tier, and the cheap station-hotel night just moves from the 26th to the 27th.

### 30 Oct Chuzenji day trip ✅ VERIFIED — go early, come back late

Tobu bus, Tobu Nikko Station → Chuzenji-onsen, **~50 min, every ~30 min, ¥1,150–1,950 one way**
(sources disagree; buy an area pass if doing >1 ride). Irohazaka's worst window is **09:00–15:00**;
jams reach 2–3 h in peak colour and **buses are not exempt**.

**Shape: bus out by 08:00–08:30, return bus at ~16:00, back in town ~16:50–17:30.** Do NOT
depart Chuzenji mid-afternoon — that lands inside the jam. Cost: a ~9–10 h day.

## 26 Oct — Noboribetsu day stop (RESEARCHED 2026-07-30)
