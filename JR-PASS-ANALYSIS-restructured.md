## 2. PASS PRODUCTS AVAILABLE (verified Aug 2026)

### ⚠️ CRITICAL FINDING: ALL passes are now CONSECUTIVE days

The old flexible-day passes (JR East Tohoku Area, JR East Nagano-Niigata) were **discontinued March 13, 2026**. Their replacement and all other regional passes use consecutive days only. This eliminates the flexible-day advantage that would have been perfect for this itinerary's idle days.

| Pass | Price pp | Days | Type | Covers Tokyo? | Covers Hokkaido? | Covers N'EX? |
|------|----------|------|------|---------------|-----------------|--------------|
| JR Tohoku-South Hokkaido | ¥32,000 | 6 consec | Regional | ❌ NO (Tohoku only) | ✅ Yes (south) | ❌ No |
| JR East-South Hokkaido | ¥40,000 | 6 consec | Regional | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes (south) | ✅ Yes |
| JR EAST PASS (new unified) | ¥35,000 / ¥50,000 | 5 / 10 consec | Regional | ✅ Yes | ❌ No | ✅ Yes |
| Japan Rail Pass | ¥50,000 | 7 consec | National | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |

**Key distinctions:**
- **Tohoku-South Hokkaido (¥32,000):** Cheapest. Covers Hokkaido + Tohoku. Does NOT cover Tokyo/Kanto. You CANNOT ride Sendai→Tokyo on this pass. Southern boundary = Fukushima Prefecture.
- **East-South Hokkaido (¥40,000):** Covers EVERYTHING in this itinerary (Hokkaido + Tohoku + Kanto incl. Narita Express). Free seat reservations on all trains. This is the sweet-spot product.
- **New JR EAST PASS (¥35,000/5d):** Covers Kanto + Tohoku but NOT Hokkaido. Useless for this trip's Hokkaido legs.
- **National Pass (¥50,000/7d):** Covers everything everywhere but ¥10,000 more than East-South Hokkaido for no real benefit here.

### ⚠️ CORRECTION 1 — the €190 saving is overstated for the CHOSEN itinerary

§6 and §8 compute the saving against an all-cash baseline that includes an Aizu-Wakamatsu +
Naruko branch (¥14,900 pp). The Tohoku shape actually chosen is **Shape A: Sendai base,

Hiraizumi RT ≈ ¥7,400 pp (Sendai→Ichinoseki shinkansen + Ichinoseki→Hiraizumi local) is an

### ⚠️ CORRECTION 2 — the ¥32,000 vs ¥40,000 gap is much narrower than §6 shows

§6 charges the ¥32,000 pass the FULL ¥11,410 for Sendai→Tokyo. Wrong mechanism: the pass area
extends to Fukushima Prefecture, so Sendai→Fukushima IS covered and only the out-of-area portion
(Fukushima→Tokyo, roughly ¥8,000-9,000 pp — ESTIMATE) is payable. That puts the ¥32,000 route at
roughly ¥44,700 pp vs ¥44,220 pp — effectively a dead heat, inside estimation error.

Recommendation still the ¥40,000 East-South Hokkaido pass, but on SIMPLICITY not price:
no boundary fare adjustment, free reservations throughout, and it covers JR East local lines in
Tokyo on 31 Oct (Halloween day) which the Tohoku pass does not.

### ⚠️ OMISSION — the pass covers a Tobu route to Nikko (considered, rejected)

§1 lists the Tobu Nikko trip as "NOT JR, paid separately regardless of pass". Incomplete. The
East-South Hokkaido pass explicitly covers, per validity item 3:
  - Limited express trains between **Shinjuku** and Tobu-Nikko / Kinugawa Onsen
  - Local trains between Tobu-Nikko, Shimo-Imaichi and Kinugawa Onsen

So a Nikko approach IS pass-eligible — but only via the JR–Tobu through service from **Shinjuku**,
not the Asakusa Tobu route. Capturing it would need a window reaching 2 Nov (e.g. 28 Oct-2 Nov),
which sacrifices both Hokuto legs (¥12,680 pp) to gain ~¥3,500 pp. Net worse by ~¥9,000 pp.

**26-31 Oct window stands.** Independent of the pass, the Shinjuku→Tobu-Nikko through limited
express is a valid alternative to the Asakusa route — relevant if the Tokyo hotel is west-side.
