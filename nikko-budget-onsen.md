Multiple aggregator sources confirm a **free shuttle bus** to/from Tobu Nikko Station:

**Jalan says**: "Pickup Service: None" — BUT the Japanese access section reads: "JR日光・東武日光→車で5分／バス約5分（丸美下車）～徒歩1分" (5 min by car from JR/Tobu Nikko, or bus ~5 min to Marumi stop + 1 min walk). The Jalan "Pickup Service: None" may mean no station pickup on demand, but a scheduled shuttle does exist per multiple OTA descriptions.

**Confidence**: HIGH that a shuttle exists (3 independent OTA sources). Unconfirmed whether it runs early enough for 08:52 departure (hotel checkout is 10:00). Contact the hotel to confirm morning shuttle schedule.

**Distance**: 1.5 km from Tobu Nikko Station (~18-20 min walk uphill, 15 min downhill with luggage).

## 3. Day-Use Private Onsen (Higaeri Kashikiri)

### AKEBI (離れの湯 あけび) — Kinugawa area

| Attribute | Details |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Standalone day-use private onsen facility. 10 private cottage-style rooms along Kinugawa River, each with own hot spring bath (hinoki cypress or stone). |
| **Price** | **¥3,800 per room per hour** + additional charges per 30 min thereafter |
| **Address** | 19-26 Hara, Kosagoe, Nikko-shi, Tochigi 321-2525 |
| **Location** | Kinugawa area — **NOT Nikko town**. ~20 km from Tobu Nikko Station. Near Kinugawa Onsen Station. |
| **Access from Nikko** | Would need train Tobu Nikko → Kinugawa Onsen (~30 min) or taxi (~25 min, ~¥5,000+) |
| **Tattoo** | Private rooms = tattoos fine |
| **Reservation** | Required by phone (call twice: booking + confirmation). Cash only. No vending machines. |
| **Verdict** | Excellent option BUT logistically inconvenient from Nikko town. Better if staying near Kinugawa. |
| **Source** | [Navitime](https://japantravel.navitime.com/en/area/jp/spot/02301-2900385/), [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1230914/akebi-onsen), [DigJapan](https://digjapan.travel/en/spot/id=10720) |

### Nikko Town Day-Use Private Onsen — VERY LIMITED

No dedicated higaeri kashikiri facility was found in Nikko town proper (unlike Kinugawa which has AKEBI). Options:

1. **Hotel Casual Euro** itself offers "Use an accommodation on day time (without overnight stay) (charged)" per Jalan — could potentially book a day-use room with the terrace onsen. Price unknown; contact hotel.
2. **Hotel Seikoen** — kashikiri rotenburo bookable, but calling to ask if day-use guests can book it is worth trying.
3. **Kinugawa Onsen Hotel** — KKDay sells [day-trip tickets from ¥1,200](https://www.kkday.com/en/product/160843) but this is for communal baths (tattoo issue) in Kinugawa (not Nikko town).

## 4. Hybrid Strategy: Budget Hotel + Separate Private Onsen

### Option A: Nikko Park Lodge (near station) + AKEBI Day Trip

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Nikko Park Lodge (private bathroom twin) × 2 nights | ~¥24,000 (€146) |
| AKEBI 1-hour private onsen × 1 visit | ¥3,800 (€23) |
| Train to Kinugawa Onsen + return | ~¥1,600-2,000 (€12) |
| **TOTAL** | **~¥29,400-29,800 (€180)** |

**Pros**: Cheapest overall. Steps from Tobu Nikko Station (08:52 train no problem). Private onsen experience guaranteed at AKEBI.  
**Cons**: AKEBI is 30 min away by train — logistically eats half a day (minimum 2-3h round trip + bath time). Only 1 onsen session, not "soak whenever you want." Need to phone-book AKEBI in Japanese. Cash only.

### Option B: Hotel Casual Euro × 2 nights (room only)

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Casual Euro twin, room only × 2 nights | ¥38,160 (€232) |
| **TOTAL** | **~¥38,160 (€232)** |

**Pros**: In-room private natural hot spring onsen available 24/7 both nights. No tattoo issue. Shuttle to station confirmed by OTAs. Soak under the stars at 2 AM if you want. Zero logistical hassle.  
**Cons**: €52 more than Option A. 1.5 km from station — shuttle timing for 08:52 train unconfirmed (verify).

### Option C: Turtle Inn Nikko × 2 nights

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Turtle Inn twin × 2 nights | ~¥20,000-26,000 (€122-158) |
| Taxi to station on departure morning | ~¥1,000-1,500 (€7-9) |
| **TOTAL** | **~¥21,000-27,500 (€128-168)** |

**Pros**: Cheapest option with REAL hot spring private baths on-site. Lovely riverside location near Toshogu. Kashikiri baths available twice daily.  
**Cons**: 3 km from station = taxi needed with luggage. Baths are kashikiri (shared facility, reserved time slots) not in-room — might not get your preferred time if busy in peak October. Walk to/from sightseeing is fine; only the departure morning is a problem.

## 5. Final Recommendation

### 🏆 BEST VALUE: Turtle Inn Nikko + morning taxi

**~¥22,000-28,000 for 2 nights (€134-170)** including taxi to station.

- Real natural hot spring kashikiri baths (2 baths, reserved for your exclusive use)
- Walking distance to Toshogu (the trip's #1 priority)
- Budget-friendly: roughly HALF the cost of Casual Euro
- Morning taxi to Tobu Nikko is cheap (~¥1,000) and solves the 08:52 departure problem
- Tattoos: kashikiri = private = no issue

### 🥈 BEST COMFORT: Hotel Casual Euro (room only)

**¥38,160 for 2 nights (€232)**

- Private terrace onsen in your own room, 24/7, natural hot spring
- Free shuttle to station (verify AM schedule)
- Higher cost but zero hassle — the premium over Turtle Inn buys unlimited soak time

### ⚠️ Open actions for you

1. **Phone Casual Euro** (tel on Jalan/Booking page) and ask: (a) exact rate for Oct 29-30 twin room-only, (b) shuttle bus morning schedule — does it depart before 08:30?, (c) is the ¥11,000 "Others" room still available for Oct dates?
2. **Phone Turtle Inn Nikko** and ask: (a) availability Oct 29-30, (b) kashikiri bath reservation system (is it guaranteed or first-come?), (c) any issue with tattoos in the kashikiri bath
3. **Phone AKEBI** (if doing hybrid Option A): reservation for Oct 29 or 30, 1-hour slot
