# Tokyo 7-Night Base (4–10 Nov 2026) — Neighbourhood Research

## Verdict

**Monzen-Nakacho (門前仲町)** is the single best base for this trip. It delivers the densest walkable cluster of genuinely local izakaya/standing bars in a single compact area, excellent rail connectivity (5 min Tokyo Station for Izu, 15 min Shinjuku for Azusa, 11 min Asakusa for Tobu), budget Tokyu Stay apart-hotel with in-room washer-dryer at ¥10,000–15,000/night, and clean Narita access via Access Express from nearby Nihonbashi (75 min, ¥1,300). The weekend-closure problem that plagues Jimbocho/Kanda (the other top contender) does not exist here — Mon-Naka's izakaya serve the local residential community and open 7 days. Use weekend evenings for excursions to Akabane/Kitasenju when you want a change of scenery.

**Ranked top 3:**
1. **Monzen-Nakacho** — best all-round (izakaya density + connectivity + accommodation + no weekend gap)
2. **Jimbocho/Kanda** — strongest せんべろ scene but dead on weekends (Sat/Sun closures)
3. **Kitasenju** — legendary sen-bero but far north, poor Narita access, limited shopping

---

## Seasonal fit (24 Oct – 11 Nov 2026)

November in Tokyo: 12–18°C, low rainfall, comfortable walking weather. Autumn leaves not yet peak in central Tokyo (mid-Nov). No seasonal closures affect izakaya. Culture Day (3 Nov) is the only holiday — you arrive the day after. ⚠️ VERIFY: No major events blocking accommodation in early November.

## Neighbourhood Evaluations

### 1. MONZEN-NAKACHO / KIYOSUMI-SHIRAKAWA (門前仲町・清澄白河) ★★★★★

**Izakaya scene:** Classic shitamachi (old downtown). The streets behind Tomioka Hachimangū shrine are packed with standing bars, robatayaki, and old-style izakaya catering to locals. Virtually zero foreign tourists in drinking spots. "Mon-Naka" is consistently cited by Japanese food bloggers as one of Tokyo's authentic drinking neighbourhoods [1][2].

| Bar | Style | Couple budget | Notes |
|-----|-------|---------------|-------|
| 大衆酒場 魚三酒場 (Uosan Sakaba) | Taishu-sakaba, seafood | ¥4,000–6,000 | Legendary since 1949. Queue-worthy. Opens 16:00 |
| 立呑み たきおか (Tachinomi Takioka) | Standing bar | ¥2,000–3,500 | Cheap standing drinks, packed with locals |
| 折原商店 (Orihara Shōten) | Kakuuchi (liquor shop standing bar) | ¥1,500–2,500 | Buy a bottle, drink at the counter. Peak authenticity |
| 大林 (Ōbayashi) | Yakitori izakaya | ¥4,000–6,000 | Charcoal-grilled chicken, local crowd |
| 辰巳新道 (Tatsumi Shindō) | Yokocho alley | ¥3,000–5,000 | Entire alley of tiny 5-seat bars. Pure shitamachi |

**Walkability:** ★★★★★ — Dense cluster within 5-min walk of Monzen-Nakacho station. Tatsumi Shindō yokocho is a single concentrated alley. Additional options extend toward Kiyosumi-Shirakawa (10-min walk).

**Rail connectivity:**
| Destination | Time | Route |
|---|---|---|
| Tokyo Station | 5 min | Tozai Line (2 stops) |
| Shinjuku | 15 min | Tozai → Otemachi transfer → Marunouchi/Chūō |
| Asakusa (Tobu) | 11 min | Oedo Line direct |
| Matsumoto (Azusa) | Shinjuku 15 min + Azusa 2h25 |
| Izu (Odoriko) | Tokyo Stn 5 min + Odoriko 2h |

**Shopping:** Kiyosumi-Shirakawa has curated coffee shops and lifestyle stores. Nihonbashi Mitsukoshi (10 min). Ginza (15 min). Not a fashion district itself — use as a base and train out.

**Accommodation:**
- **Tokyu Stay Monzen-Nakacho** ⭐ — ¥10,000–15,000/night (€63–94). In-room washer-dryer, kitchenette, 3 min to station [3]
- **Toyoko Inn Monzen-Nakacho Eitaibashi** — ¥8,500–11,000/night (€53–69). Budget baseline, free breakfast [4]
- Airbnb apartments: ¥10,000–16,000/night with full kitchen + washer

**Narita access:** Nihonbashi (1 Tozai stop) → Access Narita bus ¥1,300 (75 min). Alternative: Tozai to Nippori (20 min) → Skyliner ¥2,520 (36 min). Total ~70–80 min door-to-gate.

**Downsides:** ① Not glamorous — working-class, no Instagram appeal ② Limited nightlife beyond izakaya (no clubs/bars past midnight) ③ Shopping requires a train ride ④ Some bars tiny (5–8 seats, no reservation = may queue)

### 2. JIMBOCHO / KANDA / YUSHIMA (神保町・神田・湯島) ★★★★★

**Izakaya scene:** One of Tokyo's strongest authentic せんべろ (¥1,000 drinking) areas. The bookshop/university quarter attracts salarimen, academics, and students. Tabelog lists 20+ dedicated せんべろ spots in Jimbocho alone [5].

| Bar | Style | Couple budget | Notes |
|-----|-------|---------------|-------|
| あつ盛 (Atsumori) | Standing bar | ¥3,000–4,000 | Sashimi ¥500, lemon sour ¥400. Tabelog 3.46 [6] |
| スタンドヒーロー (Stand Hero) | Kushiage standing | ¥3,000–5,000 | 立ち飲み百名店 2025. Skewers from ¥80 [5] |
| 魚熊鮮魚店と立ち飲みぼてふり (Botefuri) | Fish standing bar | ¥3,000–4,000 | Fishmonger by day, bar by night. Tabelog 3.48 [7] |
| イチゴー (Ichigō) | Shōwa-retro standing | ¥2,000–3,000 | Most dishes ¥150 uniform. せんべろ set ¥1,000 [5] |
| 兵六 (Hyōroku) | Classic izakaya | ¥4,000–6,000 | Shōwa-era atmosphere [8] |

**Walkability:** ★★★★★ — Bars cluster densely within 10 min of Jimbocho Station along Sendai-dōri and back alleys. Easy 3–4 bar hops per evening.

**Rail connectivity:**
| Destination | Time | Route |
|---|---|---|
| Tokyo Station | 3–5 min | JR Chūō from Kanda (1 stop) |
| Shinjuku | 10 min | JR Chūō Rapid |
| Asakusa (Tobu) | 11–15 min | Ginza Line from Kanda |
| Matsumoto | Shinjuku 10 min + Azusa 2h25 |
| Izu | Tokyo Stn 3 min + Odoriko 2h |

**Shopping:** World's largest used bookshop district. Akihabara 1 stop/10-min walk. Not fashion-focused. Marunouchi retail 5 min.

**Accommodation:** ¥8,000–12,000/night — APA Hotel Kanda Jimbocho, Sotetsu Fresa Inn Ochanomizu, Villa Fontaine [9].

**Narita access:** N'EX from Tokyo Stn (3 min away) — 60 min, ¥3,070. Or Access Narita bus from Tokyo Stn — 75 min, ¥1,300. Total ~65–80 min.

**Downsides:** ⚠️ **CRITICAL: Many bars closed Saturday/Sunday** (salaryman district). This is a 7-night stay including a full weekend — you lose 2–3 evenings of local drinking if you don't plan excursions. Quiet after 22:00. No late-night scene.

### 3. KITASENJU (北千住) ★★★★☆

**Izakaya scene:** Repeatedly cited as Tokyo's #1 せんべろ district [10][11]. The west exit's 飲み屋横丁 (drinking alley) has 50+ tiny bars in a single concentrated block. Extremely cheap, 100% local crowd.

| Bar | Style | Couple budget | Notes |
|-----|-------|---------------|-------|
| 大はし (Ōhashi) | Taishu-sakaba since 1949 | ¥3,000–5,000 | Legendary nikomi stew. Tabelog 3.60 [12] |
| 千住の永見 (Senju no Nagami) | Standing bar | ¥2,000–3,000 | Since 1947. Sashimi + beer from ¥350 |
| 天七 (Tenshichi) | Kushikatsu standing | ¥2,000–3,500 | Deep-fried skewers from ¥100. Queue expected |
| 大坪屋 (Ōtsuboya) | Old-school izakaya | ¥3,000–4,000 | Pre-war building. Sake specialist |
| やきとん ひなた (Yakiton Hinata) | Grilled pork offal | ¥2,500–3,500 | Excellent yakiton, standing |

**Walkability:** ★★★★★ — The 飲み屋横丁 is one compact block. Everything within 3 min of west exit.

**Rail connectivity:**
| Destination | Time | Route |
|---|---|---|
| Tokyo Station | 18 min | JR Jōban → Ueno → Yamanote/Keihin-Tōhoku |
| Shinjuku | 22 min | Chiyoda Line to Yoyogi-Uehara area, or JR via Ueno+Yamanote |
| Asakusa (Tobu) | 10 min | Tobu Skytree Line direct |
| Matsumoto | Shinjuku 22 min + Azusa 2h25 |
| Izu | Tokyo Stn 18 min + Odoriko 2h |

**Shopping:** LUMINE Kitasenju (attached to station) for mainstream fashion. Marui department store. Practical shopping good; no luxury or electronics.

**Accommodation:** ¥7,000–10,000/night — limited business hotels. Hotel Coco Grand Kitasenju ~¥9,000. Few chain options [13].

**Narita access:** POOR. No direct limited express. JR to Ueno (8 min) → Skyliner (36 min) = ~55 min. Or JR to Tokyo Stn (18 min) → N'EX = 80+ min. Skyliner route is acceptable but adds a transfer.

**Downsides:** ① Far north — feels "outside" central Tokyo ② Very few hotels (residential area) ③ Narita access requires transfer ④ Can feel rough/gritty around 飲み屋横丁 late at night ⑤ Limited day-trip connectivity (Shinjuku 22 min is OK but not ideal for early Azusa)

### 4. KOENJI / ASAGAYA / NISHI-OGIKUBO (高円寺・阿佐ヶ谷・西荻窪) ★★★★☆

**Izakaya scene:** The Chūō Line west of Shinjuku has a legendary drinking culture. Koenji is bohemian and counter-culture; Asagaya is quieter and more local; Nishi-Ogikubo has artisanal wine bars and intimate izakaya. Each station has its own character [14][15].

| Bar | Style | Couple budget | Notes |
|-----|-------|---------------|-------|
| 四文屋 (Shimonnya, Koenji) | Standing yakiton | ¥2,500–4,000 | ¥110 per skewer. Multiple branches |
| 大将 (Taishō, Koenji) | Taishu-sakaba | ¥3,000–5,000 | Under the tracks. Shōwa atmosphere |
| 鳥よし (Toriyoshi, Asagaya) | Yakitori | ¥3,500–5,000 | Local favourite, charcoal |
| やきとん秋 (Yakiton Aki, Asagaya) | Yakiton standing | ¥2,000–3,500 | Budget pork offal specialist |
| 戎 (Ebisu, Nishi-Ogi) | Izakaya | ¥4,000–6,000 | Natural wine + local food |

**Walkability:** ★★★★☆ — Each station has a cluster (south of Koenji is densest). But the 3 stations are spread over 3 km — pick one as a base.

**Rail connectivity:**
| Destination | Time | Route |
|---|---|---|
| Shinjuku | 6–10 min | JR Chūō Rapid (excellent for Azusa!) |
| Tokyo Station | 20–25 min | Chūō Rapid to Tokyo |
| Asakusa | 30 min | Chūō → transfer at Kanda → Ginza Line |
| Matsumoto | ★★★★★ Shinjuku 6 min + Azusa 2h25 (best of all areas for this day trip) |
| Izu | Tokyo Stn 25 min + Odoriko 2h |

**Shopping:** Koenji = vintage clothing capital of Tokyo. Used record shops. Limited mainstream retail. No electronics.

**Accommodation:** Very few chain hotels in Koenji proper. Nearest APA is in Ogikubo (1 stop). Airbnb/managed apartments ¥10,000–15,000/night. Business hotels ¥9,000–12,000 [16].

**Narita access:** JR Chūō to Shinjuku (6 min) → N'EX (80 min) = ~90 min. Or to Tokyo Stn (25 min) → N'EX (60 min). Total ~85–95 min. Not ideal.

**Downsides:** ① Few hotels ② Narita access is slow ③ Asakusa/Tobu line inconvenient (30 min + transfer) ④ Each station is small — variety requires walking between them ⑤ Can feel scruffy

### 5. AKABANE (赤羽) ★★★★☆

**Izakaya scene:** Famous for daytime drinking and one of Tokyo's most authentic working-class drinking strips. 一番街 (Ichibangai) east of station is a dense alley of izakaya open from morning. Very cheap, very local [17][18].

| Bar | Style | Couple budget | Notes |
|-----|-------|---------------|-------|
| まるます家 総本店 (Marumasuya Sōhonten) | Taishu-sakaba since 1952 | ¥2,000–4,000 | Opens 09:00. Eel, carp. 1,713 Tabelog reviews [19] |
| まるよし (Maruyoshi) | Classic コの字 counter | ¥2,000–3,000 | Since 1952. Sapporo Lager + キャベ玉 |
| いこい (Ikoi) | Standing bar | ¥1,500–2,500 | Pure budget standing |
| 志茂商店 (Shimo Shōten) | Kakuuchi | ¥1,500–2,000 | Liquor shop with counter |

**Walkability:** ★★★★★ — Ichibangai is a single dense alley, 2 min from station east exit.

**Rail connectivity:**
| Destination | Time | Route |
|---|---|---|
| Shinjuku | 16 min | JR Saikyō direct |
| Tokyo Station | 20 min | JR Keihin-Tōhoku |
| Asakusa | 30 min | JR to Ueno → Metro |
| Matsumoto | Shinjuku 16 min + Azusa 2h25 |
| Izu | Tokyo Stn 20 min + Odoriko 2h |

**Shopping:** Minimal — local shotengai only. No fashion/electronics. Ikebukuro 10 min for department stores.

**Accommodation:** ¥7,000–10,000/night — Daiwa Roynet Hotel Akabane, APA options [20].

**Narita access:** POOR. JR to Ueno (15 min) → Skyliner (36 min) or JR to Tokyo Stn (20 min) → N'EX (60 min). Total ~75–90 min.

**Downsides:** ① Far north ② Can be rowdy/rough (daytime drunks) ③ No shopping ④ Feels disconnected from "Tokyo" ⑤ Narita slow ⑥ No tourist infrastructure at all (good for authenticity, bad for convenience)

### 6. KAGURAZAKA (神楽坂) ★★★☆☆

**Izakaya scene:** Former geisha district, now upscale dining. Has some hidden izakaya in the back alleys (兵庫横丁, 熱海湯横丁), but the overall scene leans toward French bistros, wine bars, and expensive kaiseki. NOT a せんべろ area [21].

| Bar | Style | Couple budget | Notes |
|-----|-------|---------------|-------|
| 伊勢藤 (Isefuji) | Classic sake izakaya | ¥6,000–10,000 | Upscale, reservation recommended |
| 山城屋酒場 (Yamashiroya Sakaba) | Taishu-sakaba | ¥4,000–6,000 | One of few budget options |
| 的場町 (Matobachō) | Small izakaya | ¥5,000–7,000 | Hidden alley spot |

**Walkability:** ★★★☆☆ — Bars scattered in narrow alleys. Atmospheric but not dense.

**Rail connectivity:** Iidabashi Station — 10 min Shinjuku, 15 min Tokyo Stn. Good.

**Accommodation:** ¥10,000–15,000/night — Hotel Grand Palace area. Some APA hotels.

**Narita access:** Good — Iidabashi to Tokyo Stn (10 min) → N'EX 60 min. Total ~75 min.

**Downsides:** ① Expensive drinks (not budget-friendly) ② Not dense enough for bar-hopping ③ Trendy/upscale clientele, not working-class ④ French influence dilutes Japanese izakaya character

### 7. SANGENJAYA (三軒茶屋) ★★★★☆

**Izakaya scene:** Known for 三角地帯 (Sankaku Chitai / Triangle Zone) — a dense warren of tiny bars and izakaya in the triangle between the station and the major intersection. Genuinely local, young-professional crowd. One of Tokyo's best small-bar districts [22].

| Bar | Style | Couple budget | Notes |
|-----|-------|---------------|-------|
| 鶏料理 はし田屋 (Hashidaya) | Yakitori izakaya | ¥4,000–6,000 | Excellent chicken |
| 赤鬼 (Akaoni) | Natural wine + small plates | ¥5,000–8,000 | Famous among wine lovers |
| 三宿三銃士 (San-Shuku Sanjūshi) | Standing bar | ¥2,500–4,000 | Quick cheap drinks |
| 串焼 もん (Kushiyaki Mon) | Kushiyaki | ¥3,000–5,000 | In the triangle |

**Walkability:** ★★★★★ — Sankaku Chitai is tiny and ultra-dense. Everything within 2 min of each other.

**Rail connectivity:**
| Destination | Time | Route |
|---|---|---|
| Shinjuku | 15 min | Tokyu Denentoshi → Shibuya → JR |
| Tokyo Station | 25 min | Via Shibuya |
| Asakusa | 30 min | Denentoshi → Hanzōmon Line through-service to Asakusa |
| Matsumoto | Shinjuku 15 min + Azusa |
| Izu | Tokyo Stn 25 min + Odoriko |

**Shopping:** Minimal in Sangenjaya. Shibuya 5 min.

**Accommodation:** Very few hotels — residential area. Stylewood Sangenjaya apartment ¥9,000–13,000/night [23]. Need to look at Shibuya (5 min away) for chain hotels.

**Narita access:** Tokyu → Shibuya → N'EX (95 min) or Shibuya → Yamanote → Tokyo Stn → N'EX. Total ~90–100 min. Among the worst.

**Downsides:** ① Almost no hotels ② Narita access poor ③ Tokyo Station 25 min (OK for Izu but adds time) ④ Not on JR (Tokyu private railway only) ⑤ Some bars closing earlier than shitamachi equivalents

### 8. EBISU (恵比寿) ★★★☆☆

**Izakaya scene:** Mix of upscale dining and some authentic izakaya. 恵比寿横丁 (Ebisu Yokochō) is a popular covered alley of ~20 tiny bars, but it's well-known and increasingly touristy. The real local spots are scattered further from the station [24].

**Couple budget:** ¥6,000–12,000 (expensive area).

**Rail connectivity:** Excellent — JR Yamanote + Hibiya Line. 5 min Shibuya, 15 min Shinjuku, 20 min Tokyo Stn.

**Downsides:** ① Increasingly touristy (Ebisu Yokochō) ② Expensive ③ Not a budget-drinking area ④ Hotels expensive (¥12,000–20,000)

**Verdict:** Worth a single evening visit from another base, not a base itself.

### 9. YANAKA / NEZU / SENDAGI (谷根千) ★★★☆☆

**Izakaya scene:** Charming old-Tokyo residential neighbourhood. Has some izakaya but primarily known for daytime exploration (temples, shotengai, cats). Drinking scene is sparse and closes early [25].

| Bar | Style | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 谷中ぎんざ area small bars | Scattered | Close by 21:00–22:00 |
| 根津 かわい (Nezu Kawai) | Sake bar | Atmospheric but quiet |

**Walkability:** Spread out. Not a drinking district.

**Rail connectivity:** Nippori on JR Yamanote + Keisei (direct Skyliner!). Excellent Narita access (Skyliner from Nippori, 36 min, ¥2,520).

**Downsides:** ① Not an izakaya neighbourhood ② Closes early ③ Too quiet for evening bar-hopping

**Verdict:** Beautiful daytime area. Terrible for the stated priority (izakaya density). Skip as a base.

### 10. NINGYOCHO / KAYABACHO (人形町・茅場町) ★★★★☆

**Izakaya scene:** Old-money Nihonbashi shitamachi with established izakaya serving office workers. More refined than Kitasenju but still authentically local. 甘酒横丁 (Amazake Yokochō) has classic food shops [26].

| Bar | Style | Couple budget | Notes |
|-----|-------|---------------|-------|
| 笹新 (Sasashin) | Sashimi izakaya since 1930s | ¥5,000–8,000 | Counter seats, excellent fish |
| 志乃ぶ (Shinobu) | Standing bar | ¥2,500–4,000 | Quick after-work drinks |
| キタノイチバ (Kita no Ichiba) | Izakaya chain (budget) | ¥3,000–5,000 | Open late |

**Walkability:** ★★★★☆ — Bars cluster around Ningyocho station. Moderate density.

**Rail connectivity:** Hibiya Line + Toei Asakusa Line. Tokyo Stn 5 min walk. Excellent.

**Accommodation:** ¥9,000–13,000/night — several business hotels (Royal Park, APA).

**Narita access:** Excellent — walk to Tokyo Stn (10 min) → N'EX 60 min. Or Nihonbashi → Access Narita bus ¥1,300. Total ~70–75 min.

**Downsides:** ① Less cheap than Kitasenju/Akabane (office-worker pricing, not sen-bero) ② Weekend closures (some, less severe than Jimbocho) ③ Not exciting — functional but lacks yokocho charm

### 11. NAKAMEGURO / YUTENJI (中目黒・祐天寺) ★★☆☆☆

**Izakaya scene:** Trendy dining/wine/cocktail area. NOT a せんべろ district. Only ~3 genuine taishu-sakaba (チャン栓チャン, べったこ, 味のごんどう). Expensive (¥8,000–14,000/couple), scattered [27].

**Verdict:** Worth one evening visit for upscale dining. Poor choice for 7-night izakaya base. Skip.

### BONUS: Additional Strong Candidates

**Jūjō (十条)** — Adjacent to Akabane. 斎藤酒場 (since 1928) is legendary. Saikyō Line 10 min to Shinjuku. Tiny area (1-evening excursion, not a base) [28].

**Nakano (中野)** — 5 min from Shinjuku. レンガ坂 (Rengasaka) drinking alley + Nakano Broadway shopping. Good izakaya scene but getting touristed. Strong Azusa access [29].

**Keisei-Tateishi (京成立石)** — ⚠️ **Under demolition** (Tōyō Keizai Apr 2025 confirms [30]). Some bars survive but the legendary "Mecca of Senbero" is dying. Worth a farewell visit only, not a base.

## Final Ranking & Recommendation

### 🥇 #1: MONZEN-NAKACHO (門前仲町)

The best overall pick. Dense walkable izakaya (Tatsumi Shindō yokocho, Uosan Sakaba, Takioka), open 7 days, budget accommodation with Tokyu Stay in-room washer-dryer, 5 min to Tokyo Station (Izu), 15 min to Shinjuku (Azusa), 11 min to Asakusa (Tobu), clean Narita access. Shitamachi charm without being inconveniently far like Kitasenju/Akabane.

### 🥈 #2: JIMBOCHO / KANDA (神保町・神田)

Strongest pure せんべろ density and unbeatable rail connectivity (3 min Tokyo Stn!). Would be #1 except for the **weekend closure problem** — in a 7-night stay with 2 weekends, losing 2–3 prime drinking evenings is significant. Mitigation: base here and take Sat/Sun evenings to Akabane/Monzen-Nakacho. Still an excellent choice if you prefer cheapest possible drinking + best transport hub.

### 🥉 #3: KITASENJU (北千住)

Purest, cheapest, most authentic sen-bero experience in Tokyo. But far north, limited hotels, poor Narita access, and limited shopping make it suboptimal as a 7-night base. Best used as a 2–3 evening excursion destination from a more central base.

---

### If I had to pick ONE: **Monzen-Nakacho**

It's the only area that scores 4+ stars across ALL criteria simultaneously: izakaya authenticity, density, walkability, 7-day availability, rail connectivity, accommodation value, and Narita access. Jimbocho is technically a stronger drinking scene on weekdays, but loses evenings on the weekend that Mon-Naka doesn't. For a 7-night stay where you want to walk out every single evening into a local izakaya cluster, Mon-Naka is the answer.

**Recommended accommodation:** Tokyu Stay Monzen-Nakacho, Twin with Kitchenette — ~¥10,000–15,000/night. In-room washer-dryer solves the late-trip laundry problem. Kitchenette covers breakfasts. 3-min walk to station, 5-min walk to drinking streets.

**Evening strategy for the week:**
- Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri: Monzen-Nakacho home turf (Tatsumi Shindō, Uosan, standing bars)
- Wed: Jimbocho/Kanda (10 min by Metro — the salaryman bars mid-week)
- Sat: Kitasenju (10 min on Oedo+Tobu — legendary weekend drinking)
- Sun: Akabane Ichibangai (20 min JR — daytime drinking into evening)
