# Tohoku 2 Extra Nights — Decision-Ready Comparison

**Dates:** 28 Oct (arrive Sendai 13:29) → 31 Oct (depart to Tokyo by mid-morning)
**Constraints:** No car, no hiking, tattoos (private bath only), budget ¥6-12K ordinary / ¥18-33K onsen, 2 onsen nights already booked elsewhere, food priority HIGH.

---

## ELIMINATED CANDIDATES

| Candidate | Reason Eliminated |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Zao Okama (crater lake)** | Car-dependent. No public bus to Okama in late Oct. Taxi ¥10,000+ RT. |
| **Ginzan Onsen** | Car-dependent (bus from Oishida is infrequent and slow). Also communal-bath focus — hard to verify tattoo-friendly private options. Already have 2 onsen nights. |
| **Yamadera (Risshakuji)** | 1,015 stone steps is the entire point. Without climbing, nothing compelling at the base. Fails no-hiking rule. |
| **Kakunodate** | 2.5h from Sendai (Shinkansen to Akita direction + transfer). Too far for day trip. Primarily a spring destination (weeping cherry). |
| **Tsuruoka / Dewa Sanzan** | Dewa Sanzan = serious hiking. Tsuruoka itself has limited non-hiking appeal. Too far. |
| **Naruko as OVERNIGHT** | ¥36,000-40,000 for 2 (per-person pricing). Over budget AND they already have 2 onsen nights. Works as day trip only. |
| **Zao Fox Village** | Taxi-dependent from Shiroishi-Zao (¥7-8K RT). Cute but thin — 60-90 min visit, not enough to anchor a day. |

## THE THREE RECOMMENDED ITINERARY SHAPES

### Shape A: SENDAI BASE (3 nights, 0 luggage moves) — ★ RECOMMENDED

**Hotel:** Toyoko Inn Sendai Nishi-guchi Chuo (2 min W exit, free breakfast)
**Rate:** ~¥7,000-8,500/night × 3 = **¥21,000-25,500 total** (per-room, confidence: aggregator estimate from Kayak/Toyoko-Inn.com for late Oct)

| Day | Plan | Transit |
|-----|------|---------|
| **28 Oct (Wed)** | Arrive 13:29. Check in. Sendai afternoon: Zuihoden mausoleum (¥570, 1h), shopping arcades. **Dinner: gyutan** (Rikyu or Tasuke, Ichibancho). Evening: Kokubuncho izakaya. | — |
| **29 Oct (Thu)** | **HIRAIZUMI full morning** (Golden Hall + Motsuji garden). Mochi lunch in Ichinoseki (Fujisei 9-flavor set, ¥1,200). Back Sendai ~14:30. **Afternoon: Matsushima** (Godaido + Fukuurabashi + waterfront stroll, 2h). OR rest. | Sendai→Ichinoseki 30min Shinkansen, Hiraizumi 8min local. Return ~2.5h RT. Matsushima: 40min Senseki Line. |
| **30 Oct (Fri)** | **MATSUSHIMA full day** if not done yesterday. Bay cruise (¥1,500, 50min), Zuiganji (¥700), Entsuin (¥300-500), oyster lunch (¥2,500-3,500 all-you-can-eat). **Stay for Entsuin Illumination** (17:30-21:00, confirmed annual late Oct event). Return ~19:30. | 40min each way. |
| **31 Oct (Sat)** | Check out. Hayabusa to Tokyo (dep 08:57→arr 10:33 or 09:31→11:04). Arrive Tokyo for Halloween prep. | 1h30-35 |

**Alternative 29 Oct:** Swap Hiraizumi for **Morioka noodle pilgrimage** (38 min Shinkansen): wanko soba at Azumaya, jaja-men at Pairon, reimen at Pyongyang Myeonok + Red Brick Building. More food-forward, less cultural-heritage.

**Alternative 30 Oct morning:** Add **Naruko Gorge day trip** (90 min from Sendai). See the iconic bridge viewpoint (flat, no hiking), kokeshi museum + painting workshop. Return by 15:00 for a Sendai evening.

**Total cost (Shape A):** ~¥25,000 accommodation + ~¥15,000 transport (JR pass covers most) + ~¥8,000 food/admissions = **~¥48,000 for 2 adults** (excl. pass).

**Sendai hotel (28 Oct only):** Toyoko Inn Nishi-guchi Chuo, ¥7,000-8,500.
**Aizu hotel (29-30 Oct):** Toyoko Inn Aizuwakamatsu Ekimae, ~¥7,000-9,000/night × 2 = ¥14,000-18,000. (2 min from station, free breakfast. Confidence: aggregator estimate.)

| Day | Plan | Transit |

| **30 Oct (Fri)** | **OUCHI-JUKU day.** Early bus (09:45) from Yunokami-Onsen station (Aizu Railway, ¥1,050 + bus ¥550 day pass). Thatched village, negi-soba at Misawaya, viewpoint. **Pros over Shape A:** More variety (samurai town + Edo post town are unique experiences), deeper cultural immersion, negi-soba is a once-in-a-lifetime food moment.
**Cons:** Luggage move, more transit time, higher cost, tighter bus schedule, less flexibility.

## MY RECOMMENDATION

**Shape A (Sendai base)** is the strongest choice because:

1. **Zero logistics friction** — no luggage moves, no bus schedules to hit, maximum flexibility to swap days based on weather.
2. **Best food density** — gyutan + oysters + mochi + zunda + potentially seri-nabe, all within easy reach.
3. **Entsuin Illumination** is a genuine highlight and only accessible from Sendai (evening return required).
4. **Cheapest** — ¥21-25K accommodation total vs ¥28-35K for Shape B.
5. **Optimal Oct 31 departure** — walk to Sendai station, Hayabusa direct.

**Within Shape A, the best day-trip combination is:**
- **29 Oct: Hiraizumi** (UNESCO Golden Hall + mochi) — cultural heavyweight, done by 14:00.
- **30 Oct: Matsushima full day** (cruise + temples + oysters + illumination) — the showstopper evening.

Swap Hiraizumi for Morioka if you're more motivated by noodle culture than temple heritage. Swap Hiraizumi for Naruko if gorge autumn colors + kokeshi crafts appeal more (but this is thinner on cultural substance).
