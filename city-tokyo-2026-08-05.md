### 5. Jimbocho/Kanda Bars — Weekend Closures

**CONFIRMED — this is real and the Friday placement is correct:**

## (E) MISSING — What Should Be Added

### 1. Tsukiji Outer Market (築地場外市場) — still alive and better than ever

NOT moved to Toyosu. The outer market is 100% operational and arguably improved since the inner market left (more space, less truck traffic). **15 min from Monzen-Nakacho** (Oedo Line to Tsukiji).

- Best for: tamagoyaki fresh off the grill (¥200-400), fresh uni on a stick (¥500-1,000), tuna cheek skewers (¥500), hojicha soft-serve.
- **When**: Any weekday morning 07:00-11:00 (before lunch tourist surge). Closed Sundays and many stalls closed Wednesdays.
- **Suggest**: Pair with Tue 10 Nov morning before depachika — quick 1-hour snack walk.
- Budget: ¥2,000-4,000 for two (snack grazing, not full lunch)

### 2. Fukagawa-meshi lunch on Wed 4 Nov

Already in Day-Shape A above but worth emphasizing: this is a **local Koto-ward speciality** you won't find elsewhere in Tokyo. Steamed rice with asari clams in miso broth. Two famous spots:
- **Fukagawa-juku (深川宿)** — Monzen-Nakacho shop, ¥1,400 pp set
- **Kadoya (門屋)** — walking distance, similar price

### 3. Sento with private bath option near home

- **Hirai Matsubanoyu** (private bath, tattoo OK — Sōbu Line, 15 min) — ¥2,200 pp for 1hr private bath
- This is only if you crave a local sento experience without the Noboribetsu price tag. Optional.

### 4. Morning Run / Walk Route

If either of you runs or walks: the **Sumida River Terrace** (隅田川テラス) is a continuous waterfront promenade 2 min from the hotel, perfect for a 5-8km morning jog toward Asakusa and back. Free, zero car crossings, sunrise over the river.
