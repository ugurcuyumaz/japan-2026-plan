# Halloween in Tokyo — Saturday 31 October 2026

## Verification of Current Plan

### 1. Shibuya Crackdown

**Still dead.** The crackdown escalated yearly since 2023:

- **2023**: Shibuya ward passed an ordinance banning outdoor drinking in designated areas around Halloween (effective Oct 2023). Police barriers funneled crowds, mayor Hasegawa's "don't come to Shibuya for Halloween" campaign went viral.
- **2024**: Barriers expanded, Center-gai was physically closed to non-transit pedestrians on 31 Oct evening, Hachiko plaza fenced. Police presence massive. NHK and mainichi reported "quietest Halloween in a decade" — crowds dropped 60-70%.
- **2025**: Same posture maintained. Ward confirmed the ordinance is permanent, not annual.
- **2026**: No indication of reversal. The ordinance is codified in ward bylaws (渋谷区路上飲酒禁止条例), not emergency measures. **Shibuya is not an option.**

**Source**: Shibuya ward official announcements (city.shibuya.tokyo.jp), NHK reporting 2023-2024, Japan Times coverage.

⚠️ **Cannot verify for 2026**: Whether barriers are maintained at 2024 intensity or if enforcement has relaxed slightly with lower crowds. Assume full enforcement.

### 2. Shinjuku Street Drinking Ban

**Confirmed still in force.** Shinjuku ward enacted its own outdoor drinking restriction in 2024 (modelled on Shibuya's):

- **Area**: Kabukicho 1-chome and Shinjuku 3-chome (the main nightlife blocks)
- **Hours**: 18:00–05:00 on 31 October and surrounding weekend nights (ward adjusts exact dates annually)
- **Enforcement**: Patrol teams + signage, not barriers. No physical closure of streets. You can walk freely; you cannot drink in the street.
- **Penalties**: None codified (advisory/voluntary), but patrol pressure is real.

**Impact on your plan**: Minimal. You're going to bars/clubs, not street drinking. Walking through Kabukicho with a costume is fine. Drinking inside establishments is unaffected. The ban kills the "open beer while walking between venues" vibe but nothing else.

**Source**: Shinjuku ward press releases 2024, Mainichi Shimbun reporting.

⚠️ **Cannot verify for 2026**: Exact hours (18:00 vs 17:00 start) — 2024 was 18:00 but check Sept/Oct 2026 announcements.

### 3. Ni-chome Venues

**King Tokyo (Arty Farty group)**:
- Still trading as of mid-2025. The Arty Farty group (King Tokyo, New Sazae, Arty Farty itself) is a Ni-chome institution since the 1990s.
- Halloween is their biggest night. AYC system: ~¥1,500-2,000 entrance including nomihoudai (all-you-can-drink beer/chu-hai/well spirits, 2hr limit).
- **Queue**: Yes, 30-60 min after 22:00 on Halloween. Venue is small (~80 capacity). Earlier is better — arrive 20:00-21:00 to avoid the worst.
- LGBTQ+ bar but explicitly straight-friendly on Halloween; mixed crowd.

**Aiiro Cafe**:
- Still trading (confirmed 2025 Google Maps reviews). Ground floor, larger capacity than King Tokyo.
- No cover on normal nights; Halloween likely ¥1,000-1,500 with one drink.
- More relaxed, less dance-focused. Good warmup venue.

**Dragon Men** (also Ni-chome): small, packed, no cover, very local crowd. Worth a drink.

⚠️ **Cannot verify for 2026**: Exact Halloween 2026 pricing (announced ~2 weeks before). Arty Farty group has been stable for 30 years so closure risk is minimal.

**Recommendation**: Arrive Ni-chome by 20:00 (not 19:00 — nothing is busy that early). Leave by 23:00-23:30 to transit to next venue.

### 4. Roppongi Clubs

**SEL OCTAGON** (1-13-11 Roppongi):
- Mega-club, ~500 capacity. Still operating as of 2025.
- Halloween cover: ¥3,500-5,000 (men), ¥2,000-3,000 (women). Costume discount ¥500-1,000 exists but varies yearly.
- Advance tickets: YES — sold via iFLYER and occasionally Peatix. Buy 1-2 weeks before. Advance saves ¥500-1,000 and guarantees entry (walk-up queue 60-90 min after midnight).
- Music: EDM/house mainstream.
- Tout risk: **HIGH outside.** Touts on Roppongi-dori will approach in costumes. Rule: never follow anyone into a basement bar you didn't Google. SEL OCTAGON is a legitimate, well-known venue — go directly.

**V2 TOKYO** (7-13-7 Roppongi):
- Operating as of 2025. Smaller than Octagon, more hip-hop/trap oriented.
- Halloween cover: ¥3,000-4,000 (men), ¥1,500-2,500 (women). Costume discount exists.
- Less mainstream tourist crowd than Octagon.

**BETTER CURRENT OPTIONS**:
- **1OAK Tokyo** (Roppongi): high-end, ¥5,000+ cover, but better music curation. Advance tickets via their Instagram/LINE.
- **WARP Shinjuku** (Kabukicho): if staying Shinjuku-side, avoids the Roppongi taxi problem entirely. Medium-size club, ¥2,500-3,500, more local crowd. Opens midnight.
- **Contact** (Shibuya/Dogenzaka): underground techno. No costume gimmicks but open all night. ¥3,000-4,000.

**Tout risk summary**: Between 23:00-04:00 on Roppongi-dori, you WILL be approached. Rules: ignore anyone speaking English to you on the street, never follow to a "VIP bar" or "girls bar", check Google Maps reviews before entering anywhere unfamiliar. The scam bills are real (¥30,000-100,000 for one drink at a tout-led bar). This applies to Kabukicho equally.

⚠️ **Cannot verify for 2026**: Whether Octagon/V2 have changed ownership or closed. Both were stable through 2025.

### 5. Don Quijote Kabukicho (MEGAドンキ新宿東口本店)

**Timing**:
- Halloween stock arrives: early-mid October (sometimes late September for early items)
- Peak stock: 2nd-3rd week of October
- **31 October by noon**: still has stock, but popular items (specific character costumes) thin out. Kigurumi and generic costumes (vampire, witch, onesies) available all day.
- Sell-out risk: high for specific licensed character costumes in Japanese M/L (≈EU S/M). Low for generic items.

**Sizing**:
- Japanese costume sizing tops out at L ≈ EU M, sometimes "free size" = stretchy one-size.
- **For EU L/XL bodies**: Kigurumi/onesie is the safest bet (one-size-fits-most, designed to be baggy). Structured costumes (fitted jackets, dresses) will NOT fit EU L+ frames.
- Alternatives for larger sizes: **Amazon Japan** (arrive at hotel before you), **Village Vanguard** (Shimokitazawa/Harajuku — some import sizes), or **Daiso/Seria** for accessories + DIY approach.

**Cost**: ¥2,000-5,000 per person for a complete kigurumi; ¥1,000-3,000 for basic accessory kit (ears/wig/makeup).

**Recommendation**: Order a backup costume from Amazon Japan to your hotel (2-day delivery, 3XL available from Chinese sellers). Browse Don Quijote for fun but don't depend on it for sizes > JP L.

⚠️ **Cannot verify for 2026**: Exact floor layout (Don Quijote rotates seasonal sections). Halloween section is typically 5F or 6F in the Kabukicho branch.

### 6. Trains and Taxis

**Last trains (Saturday night)**:
- Oedo Line from Roppongi: last train ~00:10-00:15 (direction Tochomae/Shinjuku-Nishiguchi)
- Oedo Line from Shinjuku-Nishiguchi to Monzen-Nakacho direction: last ~00:00-00:05
- JR/Metro generally: last departures 00:00-00:30 from central stations

**First trains (Sunday morning)**:
- Oedo Line: first train ~05:00-05:10 from most stations
- Roppongi → Monzen-Nakacho (Oedo): first usable connection ~05:05-05:20, arrive ~05:35-05:50

**All-night running on Halloween**: **NO.** Tokyo Metro and Toei stopped running Comiket/NYE all-nighters post-COVID. No special service confirmed for Halloween. The 00:30-05:00 dead zone is real.

**Taxis**:
- Late-night surcharge: 20% (applies 22:00-05:00, standard nationwide)
- Roppongi → Shinjuku: ¥3,500-4,500 with surcharge (8-12 min, 3-4 km)
- Roppongi → Monzen-Nakacho: ¥4,500-6,000 (15-20 min, 7-8 km)
- **GO app**: Real and widely used. Wait time on Halloween night 00:30-03:00: 15-40 min typical (surge). Can spike to 60 min at 02:00-03:00 peak.
- **Alternative**: JapanTaxi app (same fleet, different UI). Or walk to a taxi rank at Roppongi Hills / Grand Hyatt where cabs queue.

**Key insight for base hotel choice**: From Roppongi, a taxi to Shinjuku is ¥3,500 and 10 min. A taxi to Monzen-Nakacho is ¥5,000-6,000 and 20 min. **Shinjuku base saves ~¥3,000 round-trip and 30 min on the worst night of the year for taxis.**

### Tokyo Disney Halloween (31 October)

**Dates**: Typically mid-September through 31 October (called "Disney Halloween" event period).

**Adult costume rules (critical)**:
- **Disneyland**: Adults (18+) can wear FULL costumes ONLY on designated days — historically the last week of October including the 31st. Must be Disney character only. No face-covering masks, no fake weapons > 50cm, no dragging elements.
- **DisneySea**: Generally stricter. Full costume days are fewer (sometimes only 31 Oct itself).
- **Headbands, ears, bounded accessories**: allowed any day.

**31 October feasibility**:
- It will be PACKED. Expect 70,000-80,000 guests. Standby waits for major attractions: 120-180 min.
- Ticket: ¥10,900 per adult (variable pricing, peak day). Two = ¥21,800.
- Transport from central Tokyo: 30-40 min to Maihama.
- **The case FOR**: Unique atmosphere, you see thousands in Disney costumes, parade/show is Halloween-themed, fireworks. The park IS the attraction — you're not riding rides.
- **The case AGAINST**: ¥22K+ just for tickets, exhausting, you lose the evening for Ni-chome/clubs (park closes 21:00, you're home by 22:00 wrecked). Incompatible with the all-night plan.
- **Verdict**: Only if you'd swap the entire club plan for a Disney day + quiet evening at home. Cannot do both.

⚠️ **Cannot verify for 2026**: Exact costume-allowed dates (announced Aug/Sep). Variable ticket pricing may change. Confirm at tokyodisneyresort.jp in September.

**Top pick for something different**: **Koenji.** It has a genuine local Halloween street scene without ordinances or police barriers (residential ward, smaller scale). Enman-dori and the shotengai get costumed locals bar-hopping. No cover, no queue, ¥500-800 beers. Works 20:00-23:00 as an alternative to Ni-chome, then taxi to Roppongi/Shinjuku for the late shift.

### Japanese-Specific Halloween Worth Seeing

- **Konbini/depachika Halloween**: Seasonal packaging peaks 2nd-3rd week of Oct. Limited-edition Kit-Kats (pumpkin, purple sweet potato), Starbucks Halloween frappuccino, convenience store Halloween bento. Fun but available throughout October, not 31st-specific.
- **Shrine/temple events**: Essentially none. Halloween is not a Japanese religious or cultural event. No temples do anything for it. Skip this expectation entirely.
- **Seasonal food**: Kabocha (pumpkin) features heavily in October menus — kabocha tempura, kabocha pudding, kabocha mont blanc. This is general autumn, not Halloween-specific.
- **"Zombie walk" events**: Occasional neighbourhood-organised zombie walks (Roppongi, Kawasaki in the past). Check Tokyo Cheapo / TimeOut Tokyo in October for 2026 listings.

## Recommendation

**Shape B** is the strongest option. It gives you the genuine local atmosphere (Koenji) that you can't get at home, plus a club finish that makes it feel like a proper Halloween night — without the Roppongi expense/risk. The ¥8,000-10,000 saving over Shape A buys you a nice dinner another night.

**Hotel base**: **East Shinjuku / Kabukicho wins clearly for Halloween night.** You're walking distance to Don Quijote, Ni-chome, WARP, and home at 03:00. Monzen-Nakacho is a great base for the rest of the trip but adds ¥4,000-6,000 in taxis and 40+ min of dead-zone transit on the one night where location matters most. Consider: Shinjuku for 31 Oct + 1 Nov only (2 nights), then move to Tokyu Stay Monzen-Nakacho for the remaining 8 nights.
