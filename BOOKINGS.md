## Before you fly — not hotels, but do them anyway

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| **eSIM** — Ubigi or similar, ~€15–27 for the trip | You have **no connectivity plan at all** right now. Needed for the GO taxi app in the 00:30–05:00 Halloween rail blackout, live checks on the all-reserved Hokuto, and maps in Nikko and Noboribetsu |
| **IC card** — Apple Wallet Suica, or Welcome Suica at the airport | Regular Suica and PASMO are **still suspended for tourist purchase** — you can't just buy one on arrival any more. Apple Wallet works on any iPhone XS+ and tops up by card |
| **Travel insurance**, 2 adults × 19 days | €60–120, and absent from the budget until now |
| **Check every medication** against the MHLW banned list | ⚠️ **Frenadol and Codeisan contain codeine — banned outright in Japan.** So is pseudoephedrine (Sudafed). Prescriptions under one month are fine **in original packaging with your name on it** |
| **Type A plug adapters** | Spain is Type C/F at 230 V, Japan is Type A at 100 V. USB-C chargers are dual-voltage and just need the plug shape; **hair dryers and straighteners will run at half power** |
| **"Safety Tips" app** (Japan Tourism Agency, free) | Earthquake and weather push alerts in English |

## ⚠️ Oversized baggage — this applies to you

With **two large suitcases**, bags between **160 cm and 250 cm combined dimensions** need a **free reservation made at the time of ticket purchase**. It's **¥1,000 per bag** if you board without one. Most 28–30 inch hardsides sit right at that threshold; anything under 160 cm is exempt.

**Affects two legs:**
- **29 Oct** — Hayabusa 18, Shin-Hakodate-Hokuto → Sendai
- **31 Oct** — Hayabusa, Sendai → Tokyo

**Not** the Hokuto legs (proper racks, no size rule) and **not** the Tobu express to Nikko.

Don't plan on coin lockers as a workaround — even "large" ones are about 86 × 34 × 65 cm.
