## 3. Trip shape (revised per C3 + C4)

```
24 Oct ──── Leg 1: north (7 nights) ──── 31 Oct ──── Leg 2: Tokyo base (11 nights) ──── 11 Nov
   ▲ land NRT 09:30                        ▲ Tokyo AM      + 1-2 day/overnight trips      ▲ NRT 14:10
                                             HALLOWEEN
```

- **Phase A — Arrival day (24 Oct):** fly NRT → north same day, or 1 buffer night near Tokyo
- **Phase B — Leg 1, 24–30 Oct (7 nights):** northern Japan. Must end somewhere with an
  easy morning connection into Tokyo on 31 Oct
- **Phase C — Leg 2, 31 Oct–11 Nov (11 nights):** Tokyo base. Halloween 31 Oct, then
  Tokyo sightseeing + Nikko + 1–2 further excursions. Depart NRT 11 Nov
- ⚠️ Alternative shape: split Tokyo (3n Halloween → 4n Tohoku → 4n Tokyo) — keeps Tohoku
  in play but breaks the contiguous week

## 5. Decisions taken from research (2026-07-29)

| # | Decision | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | ✅ **Onsen strategy = private in-room bath**, not tattoo-friendly public baths | Both Hokkaido and Tohoku research independently concluded ~56% of ryokan refuse tattoos; cover patches only work for palm-sized ink |
| D2 | ❌ **Drop Tsurunoyu (Nyuto)** | No confirmed tattoo-friendly policy; famous bath is mixed communal; also booked out (6-month lead, opened May 2026) |
| D3 | ❌ **Drop Ginzan Onsen as an anchor** | Notoya 15 rooms / 6–12 month lead = likely full; Oishida bus only every 60–90 min |
| D4 | ❌ **Drop Sukayu Onsen** | Explicit "No tattoos accepted"; no private alternative |
| D5 | ❌ **Drop Lake Toya as an overnight** | Flagship properties are communal-bath only |
| D6 | ❌ **Drop Shibuya as the Halloween plan** | Ward has engineered it into a policed one-way corridor since 2024; alcohol ban 18:00–05:00 |
| D7 | ✅ **Halloween night = Shinjuku Ni-chome or Roppongi** | Venue-based, unaffected by the ordinance |
| D8 | ✅ **Buy the main costume in Spain**, accessories in Tokyo | JP "free size"/L runs 1–2 sizes below EU; Sakazen (3L–6L, Shinjuku East) is the only reliable local big-size source |
| D9 | ⚠️ **Front-load big shopping before 1 Nov** | Tax-free switches to refund-at-departure on 2026-11-01, mid-trip |
| D10 | ❌ **No nationwide JR Pass** | Price rises again 2026-10-01; Tokyo-hub shape with one region doesn't break even. Regional pass or point-to-point instead |
| D11 | ✅ **JESTA not required** | Deferred to FY2028; Visit Japan Web optional but saves 20–30 min |
| D12 | ❌ **Japan Alps option downgraded** | Its best content (Nakasendo, Kamikochi) is walking — excluded by C6 |
| D13 | ⚠️ **Kyushu = highest-risk option** | M7.1 Kumamoto quake 2026-07-28; Shinkansen suspended; Aso at volcanic alert L2 since 2026-06-21 |
| D14 | ❌ **No Setouchi Triennale in 2026** | Triennial; 2025 was the 6th edition, next is 2028 |
