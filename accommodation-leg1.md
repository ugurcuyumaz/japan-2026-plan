## ⚠️ The Tokyo nights are a placeholder, not a quote

A budget business chain in east Shinjuku at Tokyo rates, with a premium on Halloween Saturday.
**Substitute the real hotel and rate when you book.** Expect roughly double a Sapporo or Sendai night.

**East Shinjuku is the researched choice for the *area*** — 5 min to Don Quijote, 5–10 to Ni-chome, so you change at the hotel and walk out in costume; and at 05:00 the first Oedo Line from Roppongi lands you back around 05:05. Roppongi skews luxury; Tokyo Station is business hotels with no atmosphere; **avoid west Shinjuku**, dead after dark. Only the property and the price are assumed.

**One hard requirement:** it must store **two large suitcases across the two-night Nikko gap** (2–3 Nov, returning the 4th). Confirm this in the booking. Nine nights total across two blocks — 31 Oct, 1 Nov and 4–10 Nov.

## Why both onsen nights work

This is the constraint that has governed every accommodation decision on the trip: **a locked door means the tattoo question never comes up.**

**Casual Euro, Nikko.** The hotel's own site states **全客室 露天風呂付** — *every* room has an open-air bath, and it is the **only** bath in the room. There is **no public bath anywhere** on the property. So the ¥11,000 category is exactly as private as the ¥19,080 Standard Twin, and saves **€87** across two nights.
⚠️ It showed **"1 room left"** when checked — the scarcest inventory at the property.

**Noboribetsu Grand Hotel.** Private bath is a **single reservable kashikiri unit**, ¥4,400 / 50 min. Reserve it the moment you arrive. If it's taken there is **no fallback** — see the upgrade path below.

## Upgrade path for Noboribetsu

All three alternatives are researched and all have a private bath.

| | All-in | Trade |
|---|---|---|
| **Sekisuitei お任せ** | €177 | **+€22 buys five kashikiri baths** (¥3,300, 15:00–23:00) instead of one. Hotel picks your room — could be small or smoking |
| Sekisuitei standard 和室 | €212 | +€57, specified room, no smoking risk |
| **Takinoya Bekkan Tamanoyu** | €217 | +€62, **outdoor** sulphur kashikiri, intimate 24-room ryokan. No in-room bath — 50 min is your only soak |

⚠️ **Sekisuitei quotes PER PERSON** — the correction that reshaped this whole section — and **has no room-only rate**; half-board is enforced on every platform. Its dinner is a **buffet, not kaiseki**. Noboribetsu Onsen has only five or six restaurants total, so half-board is right regardless.

⚠️ **VERIFY before booking Sekisuitei:** that the kashikiri baths are **locked rooms, not curtained**. Five independent rooms strongly implies locked, but the whole tattoo strategy rests on it.

**Also checked and rejected:** Lake Toya — budget hotels there are communal-bath only, and the one property with a private bath costs *more* plus a 26-minute bus each way. Turtle Inn Nikko — cheaper but 3 km out. Hotel Mahoroba — cheaper still and reportedly tattoo-friendly, but that policy comes from a third-party site, which reverses the private-bath principle.
