## SAPPORO — 2 nights (Sat 24 + Sun 25 October)

**Key constraint**: Walk to JR Sapporo Station (06:00–07:00 Hokuto departure on 26th) OR Susukino (Mt Moiwa return ~22:30, Nijo Market 07:30). Chi-Ka-Ho underground walkway connects station to Susukino (~1.3 km).

### ⚠️ Weekend pricing alert
24 Oct is a Saturday in peak autumn travel season. Sapporo weekend rates for budget chains typically run 30–60% above weeknight prices. 2026 inbound tourism is at record levels. **Book 6–8 weeks ahead minimum** (mid-August latest).

## KEY NOTES FOR BUDGET TRAVELLERS

1. **Semi-double warning**: Toyoko Inn and APA "double" rooms often have a 140cm bed — fine for one, tight for two. Always check if a "twin" (2 separate beds) or "wide double" (160cm) is available for a small premium (¥1,000–2,000 more).
2. **Coin laundry**: All Toyoko Inn, APA, Dormy Inn, and Super Hotel have coin laundry (¥200–300/wash + ¥100/dry). Mid-trip laundry at Sapporo is ideal timing.
3. **Luggage storage**: All business hotel chains store luggage before check-in (from ~10:00) and after check-out. Confirm at booking.
4. **Free WiFi**: Universal across all chains listed.
5. **Toyoko Inn membership card**: Free to obtain at check-in. 10% off 10th night, Sunday discount, early check-in for members (15:00 vs 16:00 at some properties). Sign up online before the trip.
6. **Check-in / out**: Typically 15:00 in / 10:00 out across all chains. Toyoko Inn members same.
7. **Communal baths**: Super Hotel and Dormy Inn have them. Tattoos = just don't use them. Not a reason to avoid the hotel — the private room bathroom works fine.
