*Its spring is food-salt, not sulphur; Jigokudani and the footbath give you the sulphur regardless.*

## Tue 3 Nov — TOSHOGU and the whole precinct ⭐ 🛁 *(Culture Day)*

| | |
|---|---|
| 07:40 | **TAXI ~¥800, 3 min.** ⚠️ Earliest shuttle is 09:00 — too late |
| **08:00** | **TOSHOGU AT OPENING** ⭐ — gate at 08:00 sharp, 1.5–2 h. Yomeimon, sleeping cat, three monkeys |
| | ⚠️ **Winter hours 08:00–16:00, last entry ~15:30. CASH ONLY, ¥1,600 pp / ¥2,400 with museum** |
| 10:30 | **TAIYUINBYO** ¥550 pp — Iemitsu's mausoleum, 10 min away. Quieter and darker by design |
| 12:30 | **Yuba lunch** — Yubatei Masudaya wants advance booking |
| 14:00 | **Rinnoji** ¥400 pp · **Futarasan** · **Shinkyo** bridge |
| 16:15 / 17:15 | Shuttle back. Early dinner, terrace bath |

⚠️ **The 08:00 start is the entire Culture Day mitigation** — day-trippers come from Tokyo two hours away, so crowds don't build until 10:00. **08:00 on a holiday beats 10:30 on a quiet day.** Don't drift.
**If it's heavy at the gate:** Taiyuinbyo first, back to Toshogu ~14:00.
**Toshogu is substantially roofed** — light rain is not a write-off. Kanmangafuchi is the wet-weather casualty; move it to the 4th.
