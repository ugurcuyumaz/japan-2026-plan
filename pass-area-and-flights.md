## Q1 — Rail pass area verdict (DEFINITIVE)

**The earlier claim that the JR Tohoku-South Hokkaido Rail Pass excludes
New Chitose→Sapporo or Sapporo→Otaru was WRONG.** Confirmed via japan-guide.com's
pass page (page dated March 14, 2026 — current) and JNTO's official travel-pass guide:
the pass area is explicitly "the Tohoku region... and southern Hokkaido (**as far as
Sapporo**)," which includes the JR Hakodate Main Line all the way from New Chitose
Airport through Sapporo to Otaru and on to Hakodate. There is no carve-out for the
airport line or the Otaru line — those are ordinary JR Hokkaido Hakodate Main Line /
Chitose Line segments, fully inside the pass area.

### Price and validity — both CONSECUTIVE, neither is flexible

- **JR Tohoku-South Hokkaido Rail Pass: ¥32,000** adult (children 6-11: 50% off). **6 consecutive calendar days** (midnight to midnight) — NOT flexible/any-6-days. This is a real, current price increase from an older ¥24,000/¥30,640 figure seen on some outdated pages (JNTO's own article still shows ¥24,000 with a "prices subject to change" disclaimer — ¥32,000 on japan-guide.com's March-2026 page is the current, correct figure).
- **JR East-South Hokkaido Rail Pass: ¥40,000** adult (children 6-11: 50% off). **6 consecutive calendar days**, same non-flexible structure.
- **Neither pass offers a flexible/non-consecutive-day option.** (A "flexible 5-day" JR Tohoku-South Hokkaido product exists only as a resale-site framing on Klook — that is a Klook flexible-date *booking window before activation*, not a flexible-*usage* pass; once activated, both passes lock into 6 consecutive calendar days per JR's own official terms. Don't be misled by Klook's "flexible" marketing language on the purchase page.)
- **Free seat reservations included** on both passes at JR ticket offices, seat-reservation machines, or online via the JR East Train Reservation website (eki-net.com) — no stated cap on number of reservations found in either official summary (unlike the nationwide JR Pass which has no cap either; treat as effectively unlimited free reservations, subject to normal train seat availability).
- **Purchase channels:** available online via various resale sites (Klook etc.), via travel agents outside Japan, or directly at major stations/airports within the covered area. **Also sold directly on the official JR East website** (jreast.co.jp) — buying there avoids reseller markup.
- **New passport-reader machine pickup (confirmed, rolling out 1 April 2026):** JR East passes reserved via the official JAPAN RAIL PASS reservation site can now be exchanged at select JR East ticket vending machines equipped with passport readers, instead of requiring a manned ticket-office counter. This applies to passes bought on JR East's own site; resale-agent paper vouchers/MCOs may still require a manned counter. This is fully within your travel window (24-31 Oct 2026, well after the 1 April 2026 rollout) — use JR East's own site for the smoother pickup path.

### Break-even verdict (activated 26-31 Oct, per your plan)

Using the point-to-point fares you supplied:
- New Chitose→Sapporo ¥1,150
- Sapporo↔Otaru round trip ¥3,600
- Sapporo→Hakodate ¥9,440-9,770
- Hakodate→Shin-Hakodate-Hokuto ~¥400
- Shin-Hakodate-Hokuto→Sendai ¥18,300
- Sendai→Utsunomiya ¥8,150
- Utsunomiya→Nikko ~¥800
- Tobu Nikko→Asakusa ¥3,050-3,940

**Cash total for everything both passes could possibly cover ≈ ¥44,890-46,530/person.**

- **JR Tohoku-South Hokkaido Pass (¥32,000)** covers all of the above **except** the Tobu leg (¥3,050-3,940 stays out-of-pocket). Covered-segment cash value ≈ ¥41,840-42,590 vs. ¥32,000 pass price → **saves ≈ ¥9,840-10,590/person**, but you still pay the Tobu fare on top, so your **all-in total = ¥32,000 + ¥3,050-3,940 = ¥35,050-35,940/person**.
- **JR East-South Hokkaido Pass (¥40,000)** covers every single segment including Tobu. **All-in total = ¥40,000/person flat.**
- **Verdict: the Tohoku-South Hokkaido pass + separate Tobu ticket (¥35,050-35,940 total) is cheaper than the East-South Hokkaido pass (¥40,000) by roughly ¥4,000-5,000 per person (~€21-27/person, ~€42-54 for the couple).** This *reverses* the prior file's "essentially a wash, go with the pricier one for simplicity" framing — the two-purchase route is a real, non-trivial saving, not a wash, once the actual six-question coverage is nailed down precisely (the prior analysis had assumed the cheaper pass might not cover Sapporo-area legs, which inflated its apparent gap versus the more expensive pass).
- The trade-off is exactly one extra transaction: buy the Tohoku-South Hokkaido Pass for the rail spine (Hakodate through Nikko-approach) and pay cash/IC card for the single Tobu Nikko→Asakusa ride. Given free seat reservations are still included on the cheaper pass for every JR segment, this is a minor added complexity for a real ~€40-55 combined saving.

## Q2 — Real Oct 2026 NRT→CTS flight options, Sat 24 Oct 2026, 13:00-16:30 band

**Honest caveat up front:** live fare/schedule pages for specific airlines return
either JSON-driven search widgets (no static schedule visible to a page fetch) or
PDF schedule files that redirected to a landing page rather than serving parseable
flight-by-flight data. What follows is triangulated from what COULD be independently
verified — official airline terminal/route confirmations, third-party schedule
aggregators (directflights.com, flightsfrom.com), and one concretely-dated sample
flight (a different date, same route/aircraft/schedule pattern) — not a guaranteed
live fare quote. **Treat exact departure times as a currently-plausible schedule slot,
not a locked-in flight number** — verify on the airline's own booking engine before
committing to a plan.

### What's confirmed

- **Peach Aviation operates NRT→CTS from Narita Terminal 1** (Peach's own airport
  guide explicitly states "Peach arrives and departs at Narita Airport Terminal 1
  (not Terminal 3)" — this corrects any assumption that Peach uses T3, which is
  Peach's international/older-history terminal, not its current NRT domestic base).
- **Peach's summer 2026 schedule (S26) runs 29 Mar - 24 Oct 2026** — your travel date,
  24 Oct, is the LAST day of that schedule period, so it is a normal, long-published
  schedule slot, not a thin edge-case date. Peach's Winter 2026 schedule starts the
  very next day (25 Oct), so 24 Oct sits exactly on the schedule-period boundary —
  worth double-checking close to departure that no last-minute schedule adjustment
  moved your specific flight, though this is a routine period boundary, not a known
  service change.
- **Total scheduled NRT↔CTS traffic across all 4 airlines (ANA, Peach, Jetstar Japan,
  and one more carrier) is ~123 weekly departures each direction**, with NRT-side
  departures spanning roughly 06:50-18:15 depending on the exact schedule period
  (per directflights.com's aggregated route page) — confirming multiple carriers
  do serve the 13:00-16:30 window, though the aggregator did not expose a clean
  per-flight breakdown by date.
- **A confirmed same-route Peach flight pattern** (different sample date, useful only
  as an illustration of typical scheduling, NOT your specific day): Peach flight
  MM565 NRT→CTS departs Narita T1 at 07:40, arrives New Chitose (Terminal D) 09:30,
  duration ~1h50m. This confirms Peach's typical block time (~1h50m) and terminal
  routing, but is a morning departure, not the 13:00-16:30 slot you asked about —
  Peach does operate multiple daily NRT-CTS rotations, so an early-afternoon
  departure is plausible but not confirmed by this specific data point.
- **Jetstar Japan operates NRT→CTS** (confirmed by directflights.com's carrier list
  for this route) — no specific flight number/time in the 13:00-16:30 band could be
  independently confirmed; Jetstar's own schedule tool is a dynamic booking widget
  that doesn't expose a browsable static timetable.
- **Spring Japan** was named in the original brief as a Terminal 3 NRT carrier, but
  this could not be corroborated in this research pass — Spring Japan appears mainly
  in China-route contexts in the sources surfaced; if Spring Japan does fly NRT→CTS,
  verify directly on springjapan.com/springjapan.co.jp before relying on a T3 domestic
  routing assumption.

### Baggage — confirmed fee structures (2 adults with checked suitcases)

| Airline | Included checked baggage | Excess/overweight fee structure |
|---|---|---|
| **ANA** (full-service) | Checked baggage included in standard fare, up to 20kg free per passenger (typical J-domestic allowance) | Excess charge applies 20-100kg total per passenger, max 45kg/piece |
| **Peach** | **Checked baggage is NOT included in base fare** — must be purchased as an add-on per piece (up to 20kg/piece before overweight fee; 20-32kg triggers an additional overweight surcharge) | For 2 adults with 2 suitcases, budget for 2× checked-bag add-on fees at booking, not "free" |
| **Jetstar Japan** | Same LCC model — **checked baggage NOT included**, sold separately; Jetstar's checked allowance up to ~30-40kg total per passenger across bags (varies by fare bundle), each individual piece under ~32kg | Add checked-bag fee(s) explicitly at booking; do not assume the advertised LCC base fare is the real total cost for 2 people with suitcases |

**This meaningfully narrows the LCC-vs-ANA gap for 2 people with checked bags**: a
headline LCC fare of, say, ¥5,000-9,000 one-way becomes ¥8,000-13,000+ once a checked
bag is added per person, while ANA's advertised fare already includes the checked
allowance. For 2 adults each checking one suitcase, run the real all-in total
(base fare + 2× checked-bag fee) before assuming the LCC is cheaper — it often still
is, but the gap narrows substantially, sometimes to within a few thousand yen of an
ANA advance-purchase ("Tabiwari") fare.

### Terminal transfer note (both landing at NRT T2 at 09:30 on IB281)

- **NRT T2 → T1 (for ANA or Peach):** free shuttle bus, confirmed ~10 minutes journey
  time (sources range 8-13 min depending on exact route/walkway used), buses run
  every 5-15 minutes. Budget ~20-30 min total with waiting + walking at each end.
- **NRT T2 → T3 (if Spring Japan is confirmed to operate from T3):** shorter shuttle,
  ~3-6 minutes per some sources, though T3 is also walkable from T2 via an access
  corridor (~13 minutes on foot per Narita's own walking-corridor page).
- Either way, landing at 09:30 leaves a comfortable 3.5-7 hour buffer before a
  13:00-16:30 departure — terminal transfer time is not the binding constraint for
  this itinerary; airport/security queue time and any jet-lag rest stop is.

### HND→CTS (comparison only)

Could not retrieve a clean same-day HND-CTS schedule in this pass; ANA's own
route pages returned example fare snippets from unrelated date ranges rather than

### Recommendation

**Fly ANA out of NRT Terminal 1** for the 24 Oct 2026 Sapporo hop, if an ANA
departure exists in the 13:00-16:30 window (their own booking engine must be
checked closer to Oct once fares firm up — ANA's advance "Tabiwari" fares are not
static-listable months out the way this research pass could confirm). Reasoning:
- After a 14-hour Madrid-Narita flight landing at 09:30, a short T2→T1 transfer
  (~20-30 min with buffer) followed by a full-service carrier with included
  checked baggage, assigned seating, and no LCC add-on-fee friction is worth a
  probable ¥5,000-10,000/person premium over the cheapest LCC fare, once the LCC's
  real all-in cost (fare + 2 checked bags) is accounted for.
- If budget is the overriding factor, **Peach from T1** is the next-best option —
  same terminal as ANA (no extra transfer complexity versus choosing ANA), a
  well-established NRT-CTS operator, just remember to add checked-bag fees for
  both suitcases when comparing the real total against ANA.
- Avoid Jetstar/Spring Japan for this specific journey unless their T1 vs T3
  terminal and confirmed 13:00-16:30 slot can be verified directly on their own
  booking sites — the coverage this pass could confirm for those two carriers was
  weaker than for ANA/Peach.
- **Action before locking this in:** run the actual ANA, Peach, and Jetstar booking
  engines for 24 Oct 2026 directly (both airlines' dynamic search tools didn't
  expose static schedules to this fetch) to get a locked flight number, exact time,
  and current fare — this file narrows the field but does not replace a live fare
  check at booking time.
