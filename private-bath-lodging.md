# Private-Bath Lodging Research — Nikko/Chuzenji & Hakodate/Yunokawa

Planning only. No bookings made, no properties contacted. All prices are online-published
rack/OTA rates seen on 29 Jul 2026 for stated dates — actual prices fluctuate and should be
re-checked close to booking. Constraint driving this whole doc: both travelers have tattoos,
so only PRIVATE in-room baths or bookable kashikiri (charter) baths count — communal-only
baths are excluded even when a hotel calls itself an "onsen ryokan."

## JOB 3 — Yunokawa Prince Hotel Nagisatei, Hakodate (target: Mon 26 Oct 2026)

### Room categories with private bath — confirmed via Jalan (English) live pricing

Nagisatei has **190 total rooms** (110 Western, 71 Japanese, 8 mixed, 1 other); the property
markets "120 rooms with private hot spring open-air bath — the largest number in Japan."
Jalan's live English pricing page (queried for both 4 Sep 2026 default and explicitly for
**26 Oct 2026** — prices were identical across both dates, suggesting flat/non-seasonal
rate plans rather than a live dynamic calendar) shows a clear price ladder. **Only rooms
tagged "Dedicated outdoor bath for guest room" carry the private bath** — plain
Standard/Deluxe Twin/Double rooms (city or ocean view) do NOT have a private bath despite
similar naming.

**Room categories WITH private bath (26 Oct 2026, per-room total price for the room,
inclusive of tax/service, per Jalan — meal plan not specified per line item, likely includes
breakfast+dinner at these price points given typical Jalan half-board defaults):**

| Category | View | Smoking | Price range (JPY, per room) | USD equiv. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deluxe Twin/Double, Mountain view, dedicated outdoor bath | Mountain | NS or smoking | ¥38,800–55,800 | $237–340 |
| Japanese-Western / Executive Twin, Mountain view, dedicated outdoor bath | Mountain | NS or smoking | ¥42,000–59,000 | $256–360 |
| Deluxe Twin/Double, Ocean view, dedicated outdoor bath | Ocean | NS | ¥41,400–61,400 | $252–375 |
| Deluxe Japanese-style, Ocean view, dedicated outdoor bath | Ocean | NS or smoking | ¥47,600–64,600 | $290–394 |
| Twin room, Mountain view, dedicated outdoor bath (top tier seen) | Mountain | NS | ¥60,200 | $367 |

**Ocean view vs mountain view premium:** comparing equivalent tiers, ocean view carries
roughly a **+¥2,600 to +¥5,600 premium per room per rate tier** (e.g. Deluxe Twin mountain
¥38,800 vs Deluxe Twin ocean-with-bath ¥41,400–44,400 at the same rate-plan tier). Given
Nagisatei's own marketing centers on Tsugaru Strait views from the baths, and the premium is
modest (~7-12%), **ocean view is likely worth it** for a couple prioritizing the private-bath
experience — the view is arguably the main point of an open-air bath room.

**No specific "Deluxe" vs "Executive" bath-inclusion split was found as originally assumed**
— the actual differentiator on Jalan is the "Dedicated outdoor bath for guest room" tag,
which appears across Deluxe, Executive, and even a plain "Twin room" / "Japanese-Western
style room" label at higher price points. Executive Twin with bath (mountain view) sits at
¥42,000–59,000, essentially the same band as Deluxe Twin with bath (ocean view) — Executive
does not appear to be a strictly higher tier than Deluxe here; naming may reflect room
layout/floor rather than a bath-inclusion hierarchy.

### Tram access — confirmed

- **Hakodate City Tram** (Hakodate Shiden), line to **Yunokawa Onsen** terminus.
- Rome2rio: tram from Hakodate Station to Yunokawa Prince Hotel Nagisatei costs ~$2 (¥260
  single fare) and takes **~38 minutes** total door-to-door (tram + short walk).
- Jalan's own "How to Access" field states: **"By public bus (20 minutes)"** from Hakodate
  Line Hakodate Station — this suggests the hotel's own preferred access channel is bus, not
  tram, and is notably faster (20 min bus vs ~38 min tram+walk).
- **No dedicated Nagisatei shuttle bus was found** in any source — unlike Hakodate-Onuma
  Prince Hotel (a different Prince property) which does run free shuttles from Hakodate
  Station/Airport, Nagisatei shows no equivalent shuttle service in any source checked.
  **This should be verified directly with the hotel before relying on it** — many Yunokawa
  ryokans do run shuttles that don't surface well in English search.

**Recommendation:** default to the local bus (20 min, per the hotel's own stated access) or
tram (38 min, cheap, more scenic) rather than assuming a shuttle exists.
